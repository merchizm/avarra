# Cultures and Faiths

<p class="lore-lead">The current canon establishes cultural patterns but deliberately leaves gods and religious law open for a later atlas edition.</p>

<div class="lore-grid">
  <div class="lore-card"><h3>Seasonal peoples</h3><p>Winter preserves, Summer rules through twin crowns, Autumn honours loss, and Spring celebrates renewal and debt forgiveness.</p></div>
  <div class="lore-card"><h3>Water law</h3><p>The River Throne considers source, tributary, delta and floodplain to be a single body under one law.</p></div>
  <div class="lore-card"><h3>City life</h3><p>Night Quarters, Hollow Quarters, Spore Gardens, Ember Streets, Stateless Edges and Water Gates recur across mixed cities.</p></div>
</div>
