# Silent Start

<p class="lore-lead">The age when there were no written documents, when Avarra was formed and the first great beings left their mark.</p>
<p>First Dragons are associated with natural processes, First Giants with ice lanes and empty halls, Aboleths with disputed ancient memory, and First Fey with concepts rather than fixed bodies. The First Separation at the end of the age; It clarified Fey pathways, elemental regions, and World Veins. The name of this event, from tradition to tradition, is the First Breath, the Opening of the Heavens, the Great Crack or the Nine Voices.</p>
