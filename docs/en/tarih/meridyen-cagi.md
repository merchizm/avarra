# Meridian Age

<p class="lore-lead">The era when the record order turned into an intercontinental power with the planned rise of Ordan, the unification of Asteron and the Nareth expeditions.</p>
<p>The first landings began in 96 BC; The Broken Tower, the High Crown, and the White Hearth fell by campaigns; Halvek was conquered in 57 BC. The Kingdom of Qam collapsed between 31–18 BC. At the end of this period of oppression and isolation, the Fifth Kingdom remained on the verge of Drift.</p>

## The Fey thread

Ordan’s record order again classified root thresholds as “dangerous passages” and “unregistered oaths.” But the Root Closure prevented the empire from rebuilding Damar’s old control: a tower or seal cannot replace a living threshold and words recognized on the Fey side. Every official claim to a gate is therefore a political dispute for local keepers and court witnesses.
