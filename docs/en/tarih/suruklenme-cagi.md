# Age of Drift

<p class="lore-lead">Beginning with Pale Night; The contemporary era shaped by refugeeism, pockets of stagnation, slowing currents and simultaneous crises of succession.</p>
<p>SS 0 is the beginning of the Pale Night and the Great Drift. In SS 54 the Last Glow disappeared, in SS 66 Doran disappeared, in SS 78 the Crimson Hunt appeared, in SS 80 the currents slowed, in SS 81 Doran returned. SS 82 is the Year of Kings, SS 83 is the beginning of the campaign.</p>
