# Age of Crowns

<p class="lore-lead">The age when the Crown of Thorns, Lethar, Kar-Dur, the River Throne, human kingdoms, seasonal states, and the ways of Odraka were institutionalized.</p>
<p>As cities and dynasties became permanent, religious families became more clearly integrated with law, burial, and citizenship. The five human kingdoms determined the politics of Nareth in this age; The splendor of the Age of Crowns became both goal and justification for subsequent Meridian expeditions.</p>

## The Fey thread

After the Root Closure, each state interpreted its remaining thresholds in its own language. The Crown of Thorns treated the forest as a sovereign witness; the Frost Court guarded oaths of ice and silence; the Copper Principalities recorded seasonal hospitality law. Altinsun and the Greenwater League treated gates as boundaries for ritual, refuge, and debt rather than tools of conquest. These readings show that the courts grew from local promises, not a shared capital.
