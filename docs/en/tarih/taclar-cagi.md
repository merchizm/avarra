# Age of Crowns
<p class="lore-lead">The age when the Crown of Thorns, Lethar, Kar-Dur, the River Throne, human kingdoms, seasonal states, and the ways of Odraka were institutionalized.</p>
<p>As cities and dynasties became permanent, religious families became more clearly integrated with law, burial, and citizenship. The five human kingdoms determined the politics of Nareth in this age; The splendor of the Age of Crowns became both goal and justification for subsequent Meridian expeditions.</p>
