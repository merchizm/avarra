# Age of First Awakenings

<p class="lore-lead">Ancient humanoid societies were differentiated by environment, fey pact and magic; The age when the first settlement and usage boundaries were formed.</p>
<p>Early dwarven communities listened to stone halls; The Nimrun narrative preserved the birth from nine echoes; Kobolds specialized in air, water, and narrow tunnel engineering. Elven forms were born not in a single split, but in long transformations around forest, star, curtain, season, and sea. Human, Lizardfolk, Tortle, Centaur, Minotaur, Dragonborn, Firbolg, Halfling, Gnome and Goblin societies also became evident during this era.</p>

## The Fey thread

The first passages around the Echo-Root became tied to a season, a living threshold, and reciprocal words. Eladrin, Fairy, Satyr, Harengon, and Firbolg accounts carry traces of this age, but not one shared “Fey people” history. Some lived in Rootbeyond, some took root in Avarra, and some keep only an old promise to a court. The free courts also emerged as scattered communities guarding an account and a gate, not a bloodline or empire.
