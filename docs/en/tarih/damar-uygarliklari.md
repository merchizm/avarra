# Age of Vascular Civilizations

<p class="lore-lead">Led by the technical leadership of the dwarf cities, the multi-people Damar Union connected the continents through ancient means.</p>
<p>Unity; connected Dwarf forges, Nimrun observation cities, Gnome engineering centers, Genasi settlements, early Warforged manufacturing forges, and surface trading colonies. Ancient roads measured distance, air and water, light and sound; In some places, it could affect the perception of gravity and time. The union was unequal: large mines were privileged, the labor of small peoples was heavily exploited.</p>
