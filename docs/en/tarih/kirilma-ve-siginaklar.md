# Age of Breakage and Refugees

<p class="lore-lead">The age when old connections were broken with the collapse of the vascular network, the Great Closing, underground migrations and the Curtain Wars.</p>
<p>Dangerous parts of ancient roads were sealed; underground peoples dispersed into new shelters. Drow traditions were reorganized in the Curtain regions, the cities of Nimrun into their own lighthouse networks, and the surface societies into more local laws. Many of the old fears and right-of-way debates carried by modern states date from this era.</p>

The [Seven Lines Refuge](./seven-lines-refuge) in Asteron’s inner basins is one of this era’s most enduring surface legacies. The Veyran people’s emergence and later traditions of record and water law began there.

## The Fey thread

The Veil Wars of 2700–2580 BC grew from Damar’s attempt to register and garrison the gates. Rootbeyond’s free courts raised no common army: some gates were preserved through bargains, some through concealment, and some through open conflict. The resulting **Root Closure** bound passage to the same terms for both sides: without the right time, a living threshold, and a recognized oath, no gate opens reliably. See [Veil Wars and the Root Closure](./veil-wars) for the full record.
