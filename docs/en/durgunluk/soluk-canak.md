# Pale Bowl
 <p class="lore-lead"> An 80–120 kilometer area of concentrated Stasis on the eastern edge of Nareth's dried inland sea, whose boundaries change with season and magic. </p>
 <p> Close to the World Lode node, ancient human ways, and the archives of the lost Fifth Kingdom. There are police stations, abandoned villages, research camps and smuggler routes around it. </p>
