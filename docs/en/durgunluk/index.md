    <header class="stagnation-cover"><p class="lore-kicker">World Wounds · Limited Access</p><h1>Stagnation</h1><p>Not the death of life, but the slowing or arrest of change, decay, memory, and passage of the soul.</p><div><span>Pale Basin</span><span>Nareth</span><span>Player-facing</span></div></header>
    <section id="definition"><p class="lore-kicker">01 · Definition</p><h2>What stops?</h2><p>Wind can blow, people can walk, and water can appear to flow within a Stagnation zone. Yet natural processes fail to reach their purpose: a seed neither rots nor sprouts; a wound neither bleeds nor heals; a corpse does not decay; clocks run at different speeds; prayer-words are remembered while their meaning is lost.</p><div class="dossier-facts"><div><b>Change</b><span>Slows or ceases</span></div><div><b>Memory</b><span>Shared events can be erased</span></div><div><b>Threshold</b><span>Soul-passage may not complete</span></div></div></section>
    <section id="pale-basin"><p class="lore-kicker">02 · Core Region</p><h2>The Pale Basin</h2><p>The 80–120 km core on the eastern shore of Nareth’s dried inland sea is the oldest and most intense known Stagnation region. Its boundary changes with season, magic, and unknown factors. Its proximity to a World Vein nexus, ancient human roads, and lost archives intensifies its effect.</p><blockquote>Outposts, research camps, abandoned villages, and smuggler routes ring the Basin; official expedition records are not fully public.</blockquote></section>
    <section id="kingdoms"><p class="lore-kicker">03 · Lost History</p><h2>The five old human kingdoms</h2><div class="dossier-list"><div><b>High Crown</b><span>Eastern Nareth river plains · conquered by Ordan.</span></div><div><b>Broken Tower</b><span>Eastern coasts and strait fortresses · conquered by Ordan.</span></div><div><b>White Hearth</b><span>Foothills north of the Crown of Thorns · conquered by Ordan.</span></div><div><b>Reed Kingdom</b><span>Southeastern delta · broken by dynastic war and floods.</span></div><div class="is-lost"><b>Fifth Kingdom</b><span>Dried inland-sea shore · its name, heraldry, and political identity were erased.</span></div></div><p>After the first great event, neighbouring peoples reported losing the name, ruler, roads, and even some family origins of the Fifth Kingdom. Its land did not vanish; the shared record that gave it meaning did.</p></section>
    <section id="pockets"><p class="lore-kicker">04 · Scattered Signs</p><h2>Remote Stagnation pockets</h2><div class="dossier-grid"><article><h3>Akıntısız</h3><p>An abandoned River Throne town where moving water does not reach the lower basin.</p></article><article><h3>Last Glow</h3><p>A settlement at Nine Lanterns’ border whose lights can be seen, while its tunnels always return to the same place.</p></article><article><h3>Black Harvest</h3><p>A disputed Myconid region where fungi neither die nor produce spores.</p></article><article><h3>Second Morning</h3><p>A Reborn settlement near Akıntısız; some residents returned to life incompletely.</p></article></div></section>
    <section id="effects"><p class="lore-kicker">05 · Observed Effects</p><h2>Life, magic, and death</h2><div class="dossier-grid"><article><h3>Living beings</h3><p>Time sense, close memory, emotion, and a person’s bond with their own Name can fray.</p></article><article><h3>Animals</h3><p>They notice the boundary first: horses stop, birds turn, and insect sound falls silent.</p></article><article><h3>Magic</h3><p>Results detach from purpose: healing does not renew, fire does not warm, and teleportation can return to its beginning.</p></article><article><h3>Death</h3><p>Before breath disperses, a soul can become caught in the Threshold Interval; funerals use the Rite of Motion.</p></article></div></section>
    <section id="travellers"><p class="lore-kicker">06 · Traveller’s Guide</p><h2>No protection is certain</h2><ol class="dossier-rules"><li>Carry your own name and the last three days in writing.</li><li>Carry a non-magical clock, flowing water, and a germinating seed.</li><li>Compare memories with companions; do not travel alone.</li><li>Turn back when a road, conversation, or sound repeats.</li><li>Leave if animals refuse to advance, the road repeats, and wounds do not heal.</li></ol></section>
    <section id="powers"><p class="lore-kicker">07 · Political Responses</p><h2>Who is doing what?</h2><div class="dossier-list"><div><b>Crown of Thorns</b><span>The Pale Circle investigates; it may treat Closed Root refugees as threats.</span></div><div><b>Ordan</b><span>Seals incident sites and claims finds as state property.</span></div><div><b>Kar-Dur</b><span>Seeks to limit the reopening of ancient roads.</span></div><div><b>River Throne</b><span>Sees a threat to waterways and maintains a broad civilian observer network.</span></div><div><b>Lethar and the Homeless</b><span>Study star records and the trace of the Fifth Kingdom.</span></div></div></section>
    <section id="theories" class="dossier-ending"><p class="lore-kicker">08 · Unproven Theories</p><h2>Six stories the world tells</h2><div class="theory-strip"><span>Ordan Weapon</span><span>Failed Immortality</span><span>Wounded World</span><span>Broken Dwarf Seal</span><span>Celestial Punishment</span><span>Final Stillness</span></div><p>None is public, confirmed truth: an Ordan weapon, a king’s immortality rite, a wound in the world, an underground seal, celestial punishment, or the possibility that the catastrophe is salvation all compete as explanations.</p></section>
