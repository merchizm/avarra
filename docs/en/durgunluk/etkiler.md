# Effects of Recession
 <div class="lore-grid"> <div class="lore-card"> <h3> Creatures </h3> <p> Time perception, recent memory, emotions and association with the name may be disrupted; Wounds neither bleed nor heal. </p> </div> <div class="lore-card"> <h3> Animals </h3> <p> Recognize boundaries before humans; Horses stopping and birds changing direction are early warnings. </p> </div> <div class="lore-card"> <h3> Spell </h3> <p> The link between result and purpose is broken: healing does not regenerate tissue, teleportation returns it to the beginning. </p> </div> <div class="lore-card"> <h3> Death </h3> <p> The corpse may not decay; The name may get stuck in the Threshold Range. The Movement Ceremony aims to continue the process. </p> </div> </div>
