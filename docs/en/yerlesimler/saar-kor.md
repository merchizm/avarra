Saar-Kor’s social map turns inward like its caldera: ancestry, archives, and military authority grow heavier nearer the centre. The outer terraces live by pack animals, casting, and foreign trade; anyone descending inward sees that the city is not merely a capital, but a claim of right founded on dragon inheritance.

## The crown inside the caldera

**Red Steps** holds the war academies, **Ash Ring** the foundries and guest markets, and **Lineage Wells** the dynastic archives and tomb route. Nearness to the caldera centre is privilege but also danger: returning heat in the lower forges threatens the old dragon chambers and the lineage records within.

Dynasties want to read that heat as a dragon’s return; outer-terrace workers see cracked water lines and districts not evacuated. The real question is not “is the dragon returning?” but who may raise an army with that rumour.

## Table tensions

- A dragon tomb is struck from inside, while the lineage archive says that tomb is empty.
- Flamekin furnace workers halt casting until the lower terraces are evacuated.
- A student of Red Steps bears the same lineage name as a crystal memory in Prism.
