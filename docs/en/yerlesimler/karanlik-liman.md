Neutrality is geography before ideology: Dark Harbor is one of few safe descents to the underground sea. Enemies must repair ships, take water, and exchange crews here. **Four Ropes**, **Dark Bazaar**, and **Tide Court** make the price of that neutrality visible.

### Table tensions

- A warship arrives neutral, crewed entirely by citizens missing from another state.
- A deep-sea lantern signals a second harbour below the first.
- An old Tide Court ruling suddenly makes Vel-Shaar–Kor-Dum trade criminal.
