Class is visible height in Black Anvil. Nobles live close to safe air and coolness, workers close to magma heat. A broken chain is never merely an accident: it threatens who may be allowed to fall. **High Chain**, **Black Hammer**, and **Lower Ember** hold that hierarchy together.

### Table tensions

- One chain forces everyone stepping on it to sing a Kor-Dum song.
- Lower Ember finds a maintenance fault that can seal palace exits without cutting air.
- The lineage archive reveals the ruling house descends from the first chain workers.
