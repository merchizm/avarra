# Tazir

<p class="lore-lead">Tazir’s stone roofs stand close together in the dry season to preserve water more than shade. It is an old stop on the semi-arid road beyond the coast, where water is shared through open records.</p>

<div class="record-layout"><div><h2>City identity</h2><p><b>Function:</b> Cistern maintenance, caravan market, weaving, and water law. <b>Population:</b> 140,000–210,000 permanent residents, approaching 290,000 in route season. <b>Peoples:</b> A human majority with Halfling, Leonin, Centaur, Orc, and other road communities. <b>Government:</b> The Cistern Council and district water shares.</p><h2>City logic</h2><p>Cisterns at the last reliable bend in a seasonal river sustain stone houses and Dry Market. The city’s law does not claim the migration routes beyond its gate.</p><h2>Current fault line</h2><p>An unregistered well has been opened. The city must decide whether it belongs to common water or new wealth.</p></div><aside class="record-profile"><div class="portrait-placeholder"><span>TAZIR</span><small>Image pending</small></div></aside></div>

## Layers of the city

**Four Cisterns** forms the public courts, **Dry Market** the shaded exchange bazaar, and **Road House** the three-day safe stop for seasonal guests. In Tazir, everyone who uses water must know when it runs out.
