Nivarra’s archive is not beneath the palace but in the rift wall. Treaties, evidence, and state burials are set in ice layer by layer; the city was built on the assumption that cold keeps everything. As the short summer lengthens, that assumption melts, and the past arrives in the public square with the water.

## A state held by ice

**Frost Palace** stands on the upper shelf against fjord winds. **Frozen Witnesses** houses jurists maintaining evidence and burial ice. **Short Summer Market** gathers the continent’s fish, fur, metal, and seed trade for two open months. The **Melting District** is worker housing moved a little farther downhill every spring.

Frost Palace law holds that whatever comes from ice is not merely an object but a witness. Yet it is unclear which year’s law applies. If a prisoner thawed from an old war says the war never ended, how binding are today’s treaties?

## Table tensions

- A thawing seal invalidates the property claims of three living households.
- Before the fjord route opens, every scale in Short Summer Market begins to show the same wrong weight.
- Giant’s Sleep carries not the voice of a century-old prisoner, but the voices of children who spoke with one.
