# Thornsael

<p class="lore-lead">Thornsael is a Wood Elf city where old forest roads narrow and birch groves meet dark, shaded ridges. It remained within the Thorn Crown after the War of Roses, but its council has never agreed to tell its past only in the victor’s language.</p>

Stone buildings are few and living-wood structures many. The city sees itself not as the forest’s central owner, but as the arbiter of groves living in the same shade without cutting through one another. This often brings it into conflict with the Thorn Crown’s more centralised readings of root law.

## City life

**Birch Court** holds the council and common market; **Raven Arches** carries messenger roads; and **Pale Terraces** holds archive gardens and small workshops. Wood Elves are the dominant people, while Thornblood veterans, Firbolg grove families, Shifter hunters, and small human districts are enduring parts of city life.

Here the old war survives not as a monument but as an argument of authority: which grove permits hunting, which road remains open only to migrant animals, and which old burial ground cannot take a new root bridge?

## Villages of the Thornsael belt

| Settlement | Scale and function | Current tension |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Birchbottom** | 1,300–1,900 people; birch resin, beekeeping, grove care | Villagers resist cutting old boundary trees. |
| **Raven Ridge** | 900–1,500 people; fire warnings, watches, road marks | Thorn Crown command demands a permanent military tower. |
| **Broken Horn** | 700–1,200 people; hunting, leather, Thornblood crafts | Veteran families fear being recalled to compulsory service. |
| **Yellow Moss** | 600–1,000 people; marsh plants, dye, reed weaving | Is the healing moss bed protected ground or common harvest? |
| **Old Bark** | 450–800 people; old trees, small gardens, treaty witness | Root Listeners want to inspect the old words the village keeps. |

The city claims neither tax nor military levy over these villages. Its influence comes from keeping roads and markets open.

## SD 83: the cut marker

An ancient boundary tree is cut at Birchbottom in the night. Its stump bears the seals of two forest states older than the Thorn Crown. Raven Ridge watchers blame outside miners, while Old Bark elders believe the order came from within the city.
