From afar, Hal-Sur is only a white, blinding flat. The real city lies below its surface. Roads that once followed the inland sea’s shore are now cut by salt fissures and collapsing galleries. That makes salt costly to move, but forces any besieging army to face thirst and reflection sickness before it reaches the gates.

## The city beneath the shareholders

The **Hall of Shares** is the only grand building on the surface; voting power follows salt shares more than family names. Under it lie the **White Shift** workers’ quarters, **Goblin Deep** water-gathering and ventilation networks, and the **Silent Vault**, with fossils, burial salt, and sealed family coffers. Kobold diggers and Goblin water workers sustain the city while most remain outside formal citizenship.

Its most valuable resource is not salt but the past inside salt. Some galleries preserve the inland sea’s creatures; some vaults preserve salted bodies whose legal existence protects family claims. If the oldest shareholder truly shows consciousness again, not just council seats but the entire chain of property will be contested.

## Table tensions

- Before a gallery collapses, its salt veins begin writing names on the walls.
- Water workers in Goblin Deep strike to protect the only fresh-water pocket the surface council wants to cut off.
- The “dead” shareholder speaks, but three bodies in the vault are registered under that name.
