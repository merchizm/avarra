In Kor-Dum, representation begins with the shift, because workers on one magma line share air, water, and collapse risk. **First Pour**, **Cold Bridges**, and **Red Shift** make reducing one shift’s vote equivalent to reducing the lives beside the furnace.

### Table tensions

- A shift bell announces pay for a day that never began.
- Cooling water reroutes and dries a Warforged district along with noble homes.
- A worker fleeing Black Anvil proves both cities were made from the same chain.
