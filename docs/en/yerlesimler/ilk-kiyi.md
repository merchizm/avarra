## Location in Avarra

| Field | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Layer | [Seas of Avarra](../cografya/seas) · Mist Chain |
| Physical site | A low island that grows and shrinks with tide, surrounded by shell rocks |
| Access | Long Shell routes, shallow-water walks, and mist passages known only to local mapkeepers |
| Nearby ties | Thousand Ropes, Mist Chain islands, Nareth’s coast, and Tortle monastery networks |
| Life link | Rain cisterns, island seeds, and new knowledge brought by returning travellers |

First Shore’s harbour vanishes at low tide and the island’s stones become roads. Tortle travellers do not hide this change; every seed, story, or rule returned with them is held in common like the route. The monastery forbidding knowledge for the first time suggests the route itself has become unsafe.

## Memory of the road

**Shell Court** receives returning stories, **Seed Vault** holds cisterns and the island’s future, and **Mist Gate** opens only at the right tide. A traveller who lost their route has not returned empty-handed if they bring a new rule or seed.

### Table tensions

- Forbidden knowledge gives every shell in the Seed Vault the same dream.
- Mist Gate opens to a route from below ground as well as the sea.
- A returning Tortle’s shell map moves the entire island elsewhere.
