For Deep Pulse, seeing is hearing what the wall says. Small strikes from earthquake wells reveal water, collapse, distant caravans, and danger. A new regular artificial vibration may be the sign of an order spreading through the underground—or a machine waking beneath it.

### Table tensions

- The new vibration mimics the party’s heartbeats and leads to a sealed station.
- Deep Well deciphers the rhythm from Unfading Cocoon as a song.
- A wall mark announces the victims of a collapse that has not happened yet.
