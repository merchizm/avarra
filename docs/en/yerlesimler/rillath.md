# Rillath

<p class="lore-lead">Rillath is a new settlement near Wildhearth. It is not yet a full city: removable homes, common ovens, experimental fields, and irregular streets made by arriving families. Yet becoming visible directly tests Wildhearth’s way of surviving through concealment.</p>

Its founders argue that Wildhearth’s population can no longer live only through hidden districts and false camps. The first homes stand in a clearing where the forest thins and an old road is visible again. It is a good place for farming, trade, and a new life, but more likely to be noticed by Thorn Crown patrols.

## A settlement in formation

**New Hearths** are temporary household circles built around common ovens and kitchens. **Open Threshing Ground** hosts the first grain trials, while **Road Stone** marks the hidden route nearest the outside world. Rillath has 900–1,500 people, potentially doubling during harvest or evacuation.

Rillath has not decided whether it belongs to Wildhearth, will become its open face, or is a new human settlement in its own right. That uncertainty is both its greatest protection and greatest danger.

## Wildhearth arguments

- **Red Deer** sees Rillath as the first real ground of an open human-state project and wants it fortified with a visible market.
- **White Hearth** says it could prove a recognised right of use and citizenship within the Thorn Crown.
- **Road Wardens** say the open fields endanger every secret route and evacuation network.
- **Five Wardens** dispute which old dynasty’s land it occupies, seeking a right to govern.
- **The Nameless** want a city of shared labour, not past crowns.

## SD 83: the first open gate

Rillath announces its first market day. That night, an official Thorn Crown use-mark appears on a nearby tree. No one knows whether it is a threat, an invitation to recognition, or provocation from within Wildhearth.
