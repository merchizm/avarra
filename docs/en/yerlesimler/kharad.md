# Kharad

<p class="lore-lead">Kharad’s homes are cut into the wall of a dry canyon, its cisterns hidden in cool rock. Standing where the sea road divides into desert routes, the city rules not broad territory but the keeping-open of its pass.</p>

<div class="record-layout"><div><h2>City identity</h2><p><b>Function:</b> Caravan pass, cistern maintenance, stone and metal repair. <b>Population:</b> 70,000–115,000 permanent residents, approaching 165,000 in major route season. <b>Peoples:</b> A human majority with Centaur, Leonin, Orc, Halfling, and road communities. <b>Government:</b> The Pass Council, watch houses, and service citizenship.</p><h2>City logic</h2><p>The narrow dry canyon gathers rock cisterns, rope bridges, and caravan terraces. City law ends beyond the road, but closing the pass is treated as a cause for war by everyone who relies on it.</p><h2>Current fault line</h2><p>An old road collapse drives all caravans toward the main gate, turning passage rights and slave-trade convoys into one crisis.</p></div><aside class="record-profile"><div class="portrait-placeholder"><span>KHARAD</span><small>Image pending</small></div></aside></div>

## Layers of the city

**Broken Step** is the outer caravan terrace; **Shadow Wells** contains the night market and water stores; **High Cleft** holds watch houses and rope bridges. In Kharad, the right to vote is remembered together with the service once given to the city.
