# Nasima

<p class="lore-lead">Nasima’s white-stone quays descend in low steps to shallow coves along the dry coast. The city does not command the open sea like a great harbour; it survives by knowing which rock offers safety from a storm.</p>

The city treats the bay’s anchorages as common property, forming a threshold between cargo from the sea and caravans from inland.</p><h2>Current fault line</h2><p>A ship seeking shelter in a storm is found to carry year-shares and a shadow contract, testing the harbour’s neutrality.</p></div><aside class="record-profile"><div class="portrait-placeholder"><span>NASIMA</span><small>Image pending</small></div></aside></div>

## Layers of the city

**Low Lantern** holds the pilot houses, **Salt Market** the coastal trade, and **Return Courts** the names of those lost at sea. In Nasima, a ship’s safe departure is credited not only to its captain but to dock workers, lantern keepers, and the child keeping watch from shore.
