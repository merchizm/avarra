Law at Red Stream is kept in preserved blood rather than stone. The current looks red not only from algae, but because hunt, lineage, and crime evidence share the same waterway. When a Triton commander’s blood matches an old throne claim, diplomacy becomes direct war law.

## Confederacy read by blood

**First Bite** holds the war council, **Cold Archive** the blood evidence, and **Red Rift** the current gates of hunting families. Each branch governs its own grounds; the centre decides lineage, great war, and common water crimes.

### Table tensions

- Samples in the Cold Archive share one heartbeat through the night.
- A hunting shoal entering Red Rift recognises no Sahuagin mark.
- The Triton commander’s blood matches not one throne, but three claims.
