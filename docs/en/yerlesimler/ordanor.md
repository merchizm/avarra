# Ordanor

<p class="lore-lead">One of the largest and most organized cities of Avarra. Its oppression is not due to sporadic violence; It comes from the seemingly perfect order that records where a person lives, what he believes, and what magic he uses.</p>
<div class="record-layout"><div><h2>City identity</h2><p><b>Function:</b> imperial capital, bureaucracy, education, religious registration and intercontinental administration. <b>Population:</b> 380,000–520,000. <b>Peoples:</b> Veyran, human, Gnome and Hobgoblin majority; Almost every people of the empire exists as a minority. <b>Religion:</b> Plural system under the control of the Seal Order.</p><h2>Plan and neighborhoods</h2><p>The city, built on meridians and numbered rings, hides old canals and closed neighborhoods underneath. The Nine Counts, the Palace of Seals, the Fifth Province, the Alley of Embers, the City of Education, the Indifferent, the Registry of the Gods, and the Silent Meridian are separate social maps.</p><h2>Current crisis</h2><p>Representatives of the three candidates for the throne hold different wings of the same palace. General Varessa's support for the City of Training, the division of the human Fifth Province, and the Unregistered who do not appear on the official record erode the city's order from within.</p></div><aside class="record-profile"><div class="portrait-placeholder"><span>ORDANOR</span><small>Image to be added</small></div></aside></div>
