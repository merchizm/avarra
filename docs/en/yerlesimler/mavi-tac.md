Blue Crown’s palaces appear on maps not as walls but growing reef. So long as river fresh water and sea salt remain balanced, coral halls expand; a wrong dam, toxic waste, or warship anchor can sicken an entire district. Its estuary-law dispute with Namaris is therefore about the water the city breathes, not only tax.

## A capital grown from coral

**Tidal Mirror** is an air-pocket reception hall for surface envoys, **Blue Boughs** are living residential reefs, and **Pearl Court** hears cases of current and spawning rights. Coral masters, priests, and current readers all share authority because one repair can alter another’s sacred cove.

### Table tensions

- A reef gate rejects every boat from Namaris, while a human child’s voice comes from inside.
- Corals grow overnight to merge two states’ border markers into one crown.
- An envoy in the Tidal Mirror finds their reflection made decisions first.
