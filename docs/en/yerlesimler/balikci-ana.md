| Nearby ties | First Spore, Dark Harbor, and lakeside fishing villages |
| Life link | Lake purity, collective rites, and reed-farm terraces |

The lake gives no waves; movement is made by faith and oar. The more present the Kuo-Toa community’s abundance being seems, the more ordered the reeds and fish are believed to be. Its silence is therefore an immediate crisis of food and government.

### Table tensions

- Fish caught in the nets repeat decisions never spoken in council.
- Bubbles from the submerged temple spell a different goddess’s name after every rite.
- The lake’s only wave points toward the shore where a lost priest boat arrived.
