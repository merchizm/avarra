Vel-Shaar’s “street” is a bridge: every silk pattern announces which house protects whom, who owes what, or who is preparing for war. Changing a bridge’s pattern is not graffiti but a change to the law of thousands walking across it.

## The woven capital

**Main Pendulum** holds the maternal houses’ temples and courts, **Bridge Market** surface goods, and **Ash Weave** the narrow terraces of worker weavers and exiles. Houses control property and worship together; bridge wardens therefore act as customs officers, mediators, and soldiers.

A bridge forming Ash Weave marks without any weaver unsettles the entire order. If the signs are genuine, a house is secretly forging an alliance; if not, the cavern itself is rewriting city law.

## Table tensions

- A bridge pattern belongs to a maternal house destroyed years ago.
- Each ride on a fungus lift erases one passenger’s name from the silk.
- A surface caravan entering Bridge Market unknowingly declares war by carrying the wrong house’s goods.
