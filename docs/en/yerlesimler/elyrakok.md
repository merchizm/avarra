# Elyrakroot

<p class="lore-lead">A living ecosystem that was not built in a forest, but was persuaded to become a city over centuries. Its border ends not where the buildings end, but where the roots of the Throne Tree cannot reach.</p>
<div class="record-layout"><div><h2>City ID</h2><p><b>Function:</b> capital, sacred center and foreign diplomacy. <b>Population:</b> 45,000–65,000. <b>Peoples:</b> Wood Elves, Firbolgs, Thorns, and Wood Gnomes; The human, Satyr, Drow exile, and Fey communities are a minority. <b>Faith:</b> Root Covenant.</p><h2>Tiers and districts</h2><p>High Branches are the visible life of embassies and nobles; The Trunk Levels carry markets, living staircases, and crafts; Root gardens are water pockets and passages where official maps are unreliable. Crown Branches, Throne Canopy, Horn Root, Undercork, Underbranch, Guest Shells, and Blind Roots are the main records of the city.</p><h2>Government, economy, and faith</h2><p>Despite Queen Elyra, power is shared among the Crown Council, Root Listeners, Staggate Command, Underbranch Networks, and Grove Emissaries. Medicinal herbs, rare seeds, living wood, fey items, and passage shares generate income; metal, salt and grain come from outside. A new home requires acceptance of the tree; so religious authority is city planning.</p><h2>Current crisis</h2><p>As Elyra transforms, no successor has been announced. Signs resembling human names appeared on the Throne Tree, the Hornless Army demanded equal representation, and the Red Hunt targeted humans in the Blind Roots. Without intervention, the Crown Council will declare interim rule and the Crown of Thorns will be effectively divided.</p></div><aside class="record-profile"><div class="portrait-placeholder"><span>ELYRAKÖK</span><small>Image to be added</small></div></aside></div>
