# Faded Rose

<p class="lore-lead">Faded Rose is a small grove village that became permanent around the graves of those who died in the War of Roses. No one asks aloud which state a person belonged to here; the roots of trees planted in place of gravestones are witness enough.</p>

The village lives from grave care and short stays for travellers. Its people trade in both Bryneth and Thornsael, but refuse to enter either city’s arguments over history. This neutrality makes it a respected place of mourning and a site for rumour and secret meetings.

**Pale Circle** is the grave grove, **Ash Table** the travellers’ house, and **Half Stones** the field of weathered monuments. In recent years some trees flower out of season. Villagers read this not as the return of the old war, but as roots still speaking.
