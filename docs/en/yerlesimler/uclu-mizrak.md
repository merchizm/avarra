Triple Lance does not control the sea; it watches the three great mouths where the sea opens downward. Each pillar faces a different trench, and the Watch divides its fleets according to what rises from below. The undisclosed migration is a military secret to the city and an approaching disaster to nearby coasts.

## Three faces of the Watch

**First Pillar** contains command and signal rooms, **Broken Deep** shelters wounded and missing divers, and **Rising Line** controls contact with surface ships. Watch law values not driving a monster down the wrong route more than killing it; some attacks therefore look like retreats.

### Table tensions

- Signals from all three trenches say the same creature is coming for the first time.
- An injured sailor in Broken Deep speaks the language of a monster born on the surface.
- Rising Line sends every surface ship the same dream for one night.
