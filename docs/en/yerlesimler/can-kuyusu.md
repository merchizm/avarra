## Location in Avarra

| Field | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Layer | Vertical passage between Rootbelow and the [Hollow Countries](../cografya/underground) |
| Physical site | A natural shaft descending from a surface gate to an underground river |
| Access | Floor lifts, spiral freight roads, and river boats at the lower quay |
| Nearby ties | Surface caravans, Copper Chimney lines, and the Wet Deep route |
| Life link | Lift balance, shared clocks, and clean water at the shaft’s bottom |

Each level keeps a different time: sun above, shift bells in the middle, river rise below. Its freedom depends on no people monopolising those systems. **Surface Gate**, **Hanging Market**, **Clock Step**, and **Wet Bottom** make every strike a dispute between cities stacked atop one another.

### Table tensions

- A lift stops at an intermediate level that has never existed.
- All Gnome clocks fall silent while the lower city orders evacuation by river time.
- Water at Wet Bottom repeats the name of a debt owed on the surface.
