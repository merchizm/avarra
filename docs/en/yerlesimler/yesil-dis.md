Green Fang controls Odraka’s inner roads through water as much as gates. Its walls stand on marshland; an embankment opened at the right time admits one army and floods another route entirely. Red Land fires reaching the reeds are therefore not just a border violation but a change to the defence map.

## Army of the reed walls

**Fang Gate** separates military and trade loads from the river. **Wet Barracks** holds crocodile riders, rowers, and marsh trackers; **Embankment Market** supports fishers, reed workers, and migrant families around the army’s needs. Crocodile Crown commanders treat the marsh as a natural fortress, while residents know every closed embankment changes homes and fishing routes.

When fire reaches the border reeds, ash and dead roots clog the waterway. Commanders call it cause for war; reed communities call it a shared duty of clearing. Whichever voice prevails will redirect daily delta life as much as Green Fang’s army.

## Table tensions

- An embankment gate opens at night and water reaches only the commander’s political rivals.
- Crocodiles at Wet Barracks accept no rider and all stare in the same direction.
- Ash from the border fire reveals grave markers beneath an old canal.
