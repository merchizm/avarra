| Life link | The annual moon nights, rainwater collection, and imported grain |

Ayalti’s calendar aligns with the sky only on a few nights each year. Then it can meet the surface, marry, trade, or receive lost kin. Calling those nights treason makes the city’s geography itself a political crime. **Moon Threshold**, **Pale Houses**, and **Descent Silk** depend on that fragile connection.

### Table tensions

- The next moon night arrives two weeks early.
- Each knot in the descent silk begins carrying a name missing on the surface.
- A ritual delegation from Vel-Shaar declares that moonlight, not the exiles, will be judged.
