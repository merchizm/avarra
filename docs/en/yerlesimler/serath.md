Serath’s gates explain why it is not a capital: they open toward drill fields, not markets. Bread and records come from Ordanor; Serath turns them into marching formations, measured weapons, and trained officers.

## Academies, forges, and the civilian city

**Four Drill Courts** separate schools of infantry, siegecraft, mapmaking, and battle magic. The **Black Anvil** districts make armour, arrowheads, and standard machine parts; most workers are not soldiers, though military orders can alter their shifts. Around the **Old Cistern** live officer families, injured veterans, and instructors—quiet witnesses to every Ordan war.

Civil war makes every task choose a side. Families loyal to Varessa demand speed and discipline. Engineers favouring Odran want the arms route opened to trade and public works. The civic assembly tries to reduce the academies’ power over child labour, the wounded, and grain shares.

## Table tensions

- Graduates of the Map Academy give three different measurements for the same pass; if it is not an error, where did the border move?
- Thousands of arrowheads from the Black Anvil were deliberately made half a finger too small.
