# Nine Lanterns
 <p class="lore-lead"> It's not just one city; A confederation of nine Nimrun cities connected by tunnels, underground rivers, and voice communication lines. Although the ninth city is lost, its vacant seat is still in the parliament. </p>
 <div class="record-layout"> <div> <h2> City identity </h2> <p> <b> Function: </b> crystal optics, cartography, water engineering and tunnel guidance.  <b> Population: </b> 140,000–190,000.  <b> Peoples: </b> Svirfneblins of Nimrûn; Rock Gnomes, Kobolds, Warforged, and surface merchant minorities.  <b> Faith: </b> First Echo and Silent Gem. </p> <h2> Nine cities </h2> <p> First Light is the council, Deep Lens is optic, Echo Well is law, Blue Stone is water, Broken Lantern is army, Thin Bell is trade, Sleeping Ore is mine, Shadow Meter is map, Last Glow is the lost city on the border of Stagnation. </p> <h2> Crisis </h2> <p> A new sealed ballot for the Last Glitter has been revealed. The Broken Lighthouse demands extraordinary military authority; The Bluestone reserves slow down, Kar-Dur demands right of way, and the Warforged lay claim to the legacy of the old cities. </p> </div> <aside class="record-profile"> <div class="portrait-placeholder"> <span> NINE LANTERNS </span> <small> Image to be added </small> </div> </aside> </div>
