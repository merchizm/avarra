# Nisra

<p class="lore-lead">Nisra looks like a garden city from a distance. Close up, its greenery survives only along a few wells and the narrow irrigation lines that connect them. The city tries to refuse the idea that the oasis marks the limit of freedom.</p>

<div class="record-layout"><div><h2>City identity</h2><p><b>Function:</b> Oasis farming, medicinal plants, well law, and a desert-threshold market. <b>Population:</b> 90,000–145,000 permanent residents, approaching 190,000 at harvest and crossing season. <b>Peoples:</b> A human majority; Halfling garden families, Orc and Leonin road communities, and other oasis workers. <b>Government:</b> Well Shareholders and district stores.</p><h2>City logic</h2><p>Date and pomegranate gardens, thick-walled Cool Houses, and the outer Stone Table all depend on a few deep wells. A few hundred steps outside, the city yields again to dry country.</p><h2>Current fault line</h2><p>One well has turned saline and three districts face evacuation, on the same day that fugitives seeking shelter arrive at the gate.</p></div><aside class="record-profile"><div class="portrait-placeholder"><span>NISRA</span><small>Image pending</small></div></aside></div>

## Layers of the city

**Seven Shade Gardens** supplies production, **Cool Houses** holds stores and well scribes, and **Stone Table** hosts open negotiation with travelling communities. Nisra’s most valuable structure is not a palace but a well-cover that no one person can close.
