# Three Roots Market

<p class="lore-lead">Three Roots Market began as a seasonal market where three great root roads meet between Bryneth and Thornsael. Over centuries it became permanent inns, workshops, and small districts. It belongs to no city, so everyone is a little bit a guest here.</p>

Market rules rest on road law: weapons are bound, grove disputes are not opened in the market, and sellers need not say where goods came from, but must say which road carried them out. This distinction makes trade possible without reopening old war wounds.

**Three Canopies** holds the market square, **Knot Inns** the travellers and merchants, and **Underroot** the small stores opened during rain. As the settlement grows, Bryneth and Thornsael both want their own guards to secure it. The market people see that as the end of their independence.
