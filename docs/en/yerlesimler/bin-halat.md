Thousand Ropes does not sell its coordinates: it moves with the next safe current. That makes it an indispensable node of goods and news for every shore—and a power no state can easily control. Ordan’s supply-rental offer is an attempt to buy not merely ships, but a floating city’s neutrality.

## Market held by rope

**Main Knot** keeps market law, **Freshwater Line** the cisterns and water barges, and **Sail Forest** the cargo, beds, and migrant platforms. One rope links not only two boats, but two families’ debt, protection, and passage right. In a storm, deciding which bridge is cut first is the city’s hardest political act.

### Table tensions

- A rope unties itself, but its two platforms gain different pasts without separating.
- Ordan’s rental contract appears older than Main Knot’s neutrality oath.
- A barge reaching Freshwater Line reports its water carries the same sickness on every coast.
