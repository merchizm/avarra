Copper Chimney’s power is hidden in everyone’s breath. Its seven shafts distribute air, heat, and signal smoke across separate basins; one wrong setting can cause sickness or a mine fire in a distant city. **First Draft**, **Warm Rope**, and **Ash Weirs** divide its council, workers, and external smoke-debt records.

### Table tensions

- A chimney blows conversations from another city at night.
- A strike will cut the hospital’s warm-air line.
- A map in the seventh shaft shows a city missing from every official record.
