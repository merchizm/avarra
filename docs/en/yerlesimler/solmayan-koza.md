| Nearby ties | Vel-Shaar, Silent Hollow, and neighbouring-basin quarantine stations |

Unfading Cocoon measures how far Stagnation has advanced. Nothing quickly rots or grows, which inhabitants call protection and neighbours call a spreading disaster. **Ash Gate**, **Silk Stillness**, and the **Garden Without Spores** make its quarantine both border and moral test.

### Table tensions

- An unaging moth maps every basin on the quarantine wall with its wings.
- Bread from the supply tunnel never stales, but its eaters forget outside time.
- A corpse decays for the first time and the city calls it salvation.
