## A capital that moves with the water

Namaris counts boats, homes, and districts bound to its law rather than only people. Stone islands hold archives and temples but may flood another district if wrongly built; stilt quarters mark ownership, age, current tolerance, and permitted passage; floating neighbourhoods can move whole homes, shops, gardens, and courts; and underwater settlements make half the city invisible from the surface. Every map needs season and water level. An address names current, marker, hull sign, and district.

Crown Current is Samara’s mobile palace fleet, now alarming because it has remained still for weeks. Three Mouth Market is a constantly rearranged fleet of river and sea trade. Sunken Steps is the old underwater city, angry at surface taxation and underrepresentation. Dry Dock is the abandoned waterfront of people the river—and government—left behind. Silent Branch is quarantined: ripples persist, fish swim without moving, leaves do not drift, wounds stall, and underwater sound travels too far. Reed Council courts use water samples, sediment, migration, and current as evidence; Pearl Gate is the continuing boundary negotiation with sea powers.

## Flow law and Samara’s illness

Flow Bells announce water state, travel, fog, contamination, and danger. Homes require release axes, spare moorings, flotation, lamps, child belts, and current ribbons. Children learn to float, untie knots one-handed, and recognise unsafe water. Water channels are marked for drinking, garden, industry, waste, or sacred inspection.

The central law is: water may be used, directed, and shared, but never owned. Poisoning it, building unlicensed dams, permanently altering flow through magic, blocking migration, moving quarantine water, concealing floods, abandoning a neighbourhood, or sealing underwater passage are grave crimes. Penalties remove mooring, water, boat, route, and basin rights. River Mothers protect communities; the queen is elected through the Seven Water Passages.

Samara’s pulse, movement, and the palace current slow together. Bond-Cutters seek a new election, Flow-Restorers want the river healed first, and Deep Throne advocates want a queen from Sunken Steps. Ordan offers engineering, grain, and protection for a permanent military port near Pearl Gate—an offer that would militarily control Nareth’s inner-river trade.
