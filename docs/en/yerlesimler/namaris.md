# Namaris
 <p class="lore-lead"> A vibrant capital city formed by a combination of stone islands, stilt quarters, floating platforms and underwater settlements. The map cannot be used without the seasonal date. </p>
 <div class="record-layout"> <div> <h2> City ID </h2> <p> <b> Function: </b> river law, water trade and diplomacy.  <b> Population: </b> 105,000–165,000.  <b> Peoples: </b> Water Genasi, Sea Elves, Locathahs; Halfling, human, Triton and Grung are minorities.  <b> Belief: </b> Path of Flow. </p> <h2> Neighborhoods and law </h2> <p> Crown Stream, Three Mouth Market, Sunken Steps, Dry Pier, Silent Arm, Reed Council and Pearl Gate; It is as important as which branch the water flows from. Poisoning the water is treason, changing the flow with magic is an attack on state authority. </p> <h2> Crisis </h2> <p> Queen Samara slows down with the rivers. Stagnation in the Silent Arm, Dry Pier's anger, candidates for the throne and Ordan's aid in exchange for a military port threaten to interrupt the flow of the city. </p> </div> <aside class="record-profile"> <div class="portrait-placeholder"> <span> NAMARIS </span> <small> Image to be added </small> </div> </aside> </div>
