# Odraka

<p class="lore-lead">Odraka has never been united beneath one crown. Its politics turn on who controls water and where herds may pass; on the same scale, dragon heritage is both a claim to authority and an argument against it.</p>

## Moving through the continent

No route in Odraka stays safe year-round. Monsoons close delta passages, skyhorn herds change savanna routes, and the fire calendar shapes both steppe agriculture and military movement. Caravans need local council permission, water rights, and herd-passage agreements more often than a fixed border document.

The continent's central question is whether law should be centralised or remain mobile. A system of measure and record promises security to some merchants; the Endless Road and many delta communities fear that fixing water, roads, and memory in place will choke life itself.
