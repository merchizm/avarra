# Geography of Avarra
<p class="lore-lead">In Avarra, mountains are the world’s bones, rivers its veins, and forests its memory. This is more than metaphor: fault lines carry magic, old woods can hold the dead, and ancient roads can change physical law.</p>

## Continents and borders
<div class="lore-grid"><div class="lore-card"><h3>Nareth</h3><p>The great northwestern continent of volcanoes, salt basins, river plains, forests, and deltas.</p></div><div class="lore-card"><h3>Seyvar</h3><p>An eastern continent of worn interior mountains, long rivers, northern plateaus, and the Ordan Empire.</p></div><div class="lore-card"><h3>Odraka</h3><p>A southern continent of dry west, savanna, monsoon forests, rift bays, and young volcanoes.</p></div><div class="lore-card"><h3>Ilyr-Khaz</h3><p>A fractured polar shelf of glaciers, fjords, tundra valleys, Winter Eladrin, and ice Genasi.</p></div></div>

## World Veins
Ancient underways are more than passages. Kar-Dur, the Saltworks, Ordan, and Nine Lanterns each hold a different section of this network. Opening it accelerates trade, but risks carrying the Stagnation deeper into the continents.
