# Ice Rifts and Brief Summer

<p class="lore-lead">In Ilyr-Khaz, summer is less a season than a shared mobilization. When rifts open, every village recalculates food, roads and kinship debts.</p>

## Table-facing places

- **Frozen Light:** A fjord village of Rimekin ice-lamp and winter-seed growers.
- **Brief Summer Hearth:** A Goliath storehouse, fairground and winter shelter.
- **Quiet Shore:** A small Shadar-kai quarter for mourning textiles and memory chests.
- **Rift Share:** Watch huts that announce ice passage anew each day.

## Order and livelihood

Fuel is scarce in winter; time is scarce in summer. At Rift Share, passage follows the load carried that day, not family name. Rimekin share cold light, Goliaths rope work and Shadar-kai durable memory weaving.

## Pressure and hooks

**Fjord Councils** want common stocks while **Ice Traders** seek early sales. Selling supplies out leads to higher passage fees, thinner watches and villages cut off by the first freeze.

- Every lamp at Frozen Light points north instead of seaward.
- A help bell rings from across a rift already closed.
- A stolen memory chest proves one family’s passage right.
