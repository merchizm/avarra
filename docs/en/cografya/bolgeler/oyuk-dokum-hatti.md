# Hollow Ring and Foundry Line

<p class="lore-lead">In these linked underground hollows, air, light and casting order are political rights. One silent chimney can stop bread in three districts.</p>

## Table-facing places

- **Ruin Bowl:** A sheltered hollow where Ruinbound communities build new homes from stone and metal remnants.
- **Six Echoes:** Small halls where Gith mediators hear cases through returning sound.
- **Blind Hearth:** A foundry village jointly run after shifts by Gnoll and Worg worker households.
- **Stone Curtain:** A pass quarter where Medusa and Gargoyle carvers make light-breaking facades.

## Order and livelihood

Air is common, but chimney upkeep earns a watch right. Ruinbound repair, Gith mediate, Gnoll and Worg haul and track, and Medusae and Gargoyles work stone. Each contributes a distinct craft without being reduced to it.

## Pressure and hooks

The **Hollow Ring Council** backs open watches; **Black Anvil contractors** want to close the line. A broken air meter becomes a shift fight, chimney closure and evacuation.

- A decision at Six Echoes is heard before it is made.
- Blind Hearth’s casting mould vanishes before wages can be paid.
- Stone Curtain’s new face changes more than the light.
