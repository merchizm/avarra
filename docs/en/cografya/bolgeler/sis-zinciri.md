# Mist Chain and Deep Current

<p class="lore-lead">You hear the islands’ bells before you see them. In the Mist Chain, routes depend on mutually recognized beacon watches more than maps.</p>

## Table-facing places

- **Walking Coral:** A Kalamer shore village that is land at low tide and sea at high tide.
- **Shell Pier:** A small Hadozee yard for masts and nets.
- **Deep Bell:** A shared beacon for Triton, Sea Elf and surface-captain current notices.
- **Mist Share:** An island council recording each vessel’s water, rope and rescue contribution.

## Order and livelihood

Under mist law, answering a first aid call precedes profit. Kalamer offer shoal knowledge, Hadozee mastwork and sea peoples current-reading. Ports stay open to those who pay their rescue share, not to a particular origin.

## Pressure and hooks

The **Deep Bell watchers** defend neutrality; the **Private Beacon Company** wants paid routes. A false signal can become a wreck, rescue debt and a broken council.

- Deep Bell rings three times with no ship in sight.
- Walking Coral leaves an unknown chest on shore as it withdraws.
- Every rope at Shell Pier is cut with the same knot.
