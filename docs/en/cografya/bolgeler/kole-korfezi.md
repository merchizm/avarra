# Slave Bay

<p class="lore-lead">Slave Bay is the name used beyond Odraka for the sea and coast between Sâlvara and Umbraval. The name comes from the region’s ports turning human labour, lifespan, and shadow into different forms of trade goods.</p>

The bay is not one state. [Sâlvara](../../devletler/salvara) is openly a slaveholding state built on lifespan trade, while [Umbraval](../../devletler/umbraval-serbest-sehri), an independent free city, defends its own shadow registry in the language of lawful leasing. Small piers, warehouses, and salt-coast villages between them live amid the two powers’ contracts, smuggling, and wars. The shore beginning at [Nasima](../../yerlesimler/nasima), at the southern exit, is the first of the old free cities not bound to either order.

Sâlvaran officials call the region the **North Reckoning Coast**. Umbraval maps prefer only **Merria Passage**. “Slave Bay” was spread by sailors, fugitives, and dissidents in foreign states; even putting the name on a map is considered a political act.

## Table-ready starts

- A convoy of year-shares leaving Sâlvara seeks protection in an Umbraval court. The seals prove the years were taken from free citizens, not captives.
- An escape network in Broken Tide tries to rescue someone whose shadow works elsewhere while their years are held in a Sâlvara vault.
- An envoy from Merria proposes a trade treaty banning every map that uses the name Slave Bay.
