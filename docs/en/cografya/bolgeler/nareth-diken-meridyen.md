# Thorn–Meridian Belt

<p class="lore-lead">Where Nareth’s old forest meets its salt plain, a road is never only a road: timber, water, migration and passage are parts of one oath.</p>

## Table-facing places

- **Thorn Gate:** A market-fort where foresters, lumber families and travellers answer at the same stone bridge.
- **Meridian Hearth:** The last fresh-water stop for salt caravans; measures are sealed here.
- **Dew Leap:** A reed-roofed Harengon village whose council oversees garden watches and message-running.
- **Shell Sound:** A quiet woodland settlement of Bugbear households that drives off noisy plunder, not strangers.

## Order and livelihood

Water shares are written before timber is cut. At Thorn Gate, a traveller is assessed by the trace left behind; repairing that trace earns a reduced toll. Elves and firbolg keep grove watches, while other households run mills, caravans and night safety.

## Pressure and hooks

The **Thorn Crown wardens** fear overcutting; the **Meridian Measurers** fear untaxed salt. Escalation runs from a road dispute to muddied water, suspended oaths and a closed pass.

- Dew Leap’s message lines are cut at night.
- A salt caravan wants passage on Shell Sound’s sacred quiet day.
- An old bridge stone accepts passage only for someone who repairs a trace.
