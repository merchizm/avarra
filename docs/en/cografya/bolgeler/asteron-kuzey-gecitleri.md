# Asteron’s Northern Passes

<p class="lore-lead">On this wind-scoured plateau, caravan order, military control and nomad hospitality grind together in narrow passes.</p>

## Table-facing places

- **Three Posts:** A pass village where retired Giff turned a barracks store into market and training yard.
- **Far Step:** A hillside settlement of Gith sky-watchers, quiet but not closed.
- **Stone Feather:** A cliff quarter where Gargoyle carvers build windbreaks.
- **Northern Ledger:** A small post whose travel permits last only one night.

## Order and livelihood

Carrying a weapon is lawful; carrying it drawn is recorded. Giff teach road discipline, Gith maintain the sky calendar, and Gargoyles protect cliff safety. The right of way belongs to whoever serves its watch.

## Pressure and hooks

The **Northern Ledger** seeks tighter control; the **Road Table** defends common lodging. A late permit becomes a caravan pileup, food dispute and avalanche-struck pass.

- A Giff instructor says their missing artillery plans are for avalanche prevention.
- Far Step’s sky signs warn of a storm the post refuses to believe.
- Stone Feather’s windbreak has been recarved with unknown marks.
