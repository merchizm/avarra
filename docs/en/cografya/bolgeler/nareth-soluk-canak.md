# Pale Basin and Salt Roads

<p class="lore-lead">At the rim of a dried inland sea, cities govern promises of water rather than water itself. By night, caravan lamps draw a second map across the basin.</p>

## Table-facing places

- **Pale Shore:** A market town where well rights follow work rather than family.
- **Gel Caravanserai:** A Plasmoid-run inn with care pools for shape-changing travellers.
- **Green Threshold:** A salt-protected greenhouse quarter of Verdan gardeners.
- **Broken Measure:** A stone field where disputes are settled by measuring again, not duelling.

## Order and livelihood

Salt, glass and water-carrying sustain the basin. Sealed water is personal property; water in an open channel is common. Plasmoids tend caravan pools, salt dwarves store reserves and local crews audit measures.

## Pressure and hooks

The **Well Ledger** records debts while the **Salt Carters’ Union** seeks higher passage fees. A dry well means share disputes, diverted caravans, illicit digging and migration pressure.

- All care pools at the Gel Caravanserai turned bitter overnight.
- The decision stones at Broken Measure recognize only a missing archivist.
- Green Threshold’s greenhouses are about to be sabotaged before a water-share meeting.
