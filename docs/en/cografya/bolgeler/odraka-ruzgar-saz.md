# Wind–Reed Corridor

<p class="lore-lead">Between Odraka’s savanna and delta, migrating herds, reed boats and open-air courts share the same country.</p>

## Table-facing places

- **Lion Threshold:** A stone circle where Leonin tracking knowledge stands in for tolls.
- **Ivory Reeds:** A broad-house village where Loxodon water-workers build banks and piers.
- **Six-Leg Market:** A Thri-kreen market with light cargo gear and multi-watch schedules.
- **Bell Stop:** A Kender network for news, lost objects and missing people.

## Order and livelihood

The person who keeps ground passable that season speaks first, not the person who claims it. Leonin track herds, Loxodon keep banks, Thri-kreen mark dry paths and Kender keep news moving.

## Pressure and hooks

The **Reed Court** protects the old migration route; Saar-Kor merchants want a new cart-ready embankment. Opening it can cut a herd route, empty the market and divide villages in flood season.

- A herd reaches Lion Threshold from the wrong direction.
- The scales at Six-Leg Market all begin to mismeasure.
- A trace from Bell Stop leads to an officially closed pass.
