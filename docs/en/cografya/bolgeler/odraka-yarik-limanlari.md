# Rift Lakes and Harbours

<p class="lore-lead">Lakes opened by young volcanoes bind their harbours more to wind than sea. Every boat is both cargo vessel and family home.</p>

## Table-facing places

- **Flying Rope:** A Hadozee storehouse linked by rope bridges.
- **Star Rest:** A small terrace village of Astral Elf sky-readers and guides.
- **Ember Roof:** A Flamekin quarter of hot-stone ovens.
- **Rift Quay:** A shared port where Giff captains work heavy freight cranes.

## Order and livelihood

Harbour law records the mooring rope rather than the vessel. Hadozee climbing crews, Astral Elf guides, Giff freight workers and Flamekin bakers cross-schedule their watches so none can close the port alone.

## Pressure and hooks

The **Quay Council** protects common cranes; **Ash Merchants** want a private pier. A rope accident becomes an insurance fight, crane strike and a closed lake route.

- From Star Rest, a lake beacon can be seen in the sky.
- Ember Roof’s ovens go cold together.
- Flying Rope’s eldest knot-master needs help locating a lost family bond.
