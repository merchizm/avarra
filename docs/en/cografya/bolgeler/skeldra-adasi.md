# Skeldra Island

<p class="lore-lead">Skeldra is a rocky island in northwestern Ilyr-Khaz, the first land to meet the ice and wind of the open sea. Its soil cannot support broad agriculture, but its deep harbour, dry ridges, and long-winter stores make it a key to the intercontinental supply chain.</p>

The [Free City of Winter Granaries](../../devletler/kis-ambarlari-serbest-sehri) stands in the island’s sheltered eastern bay. Grain, fodder, timber, cloth, medicine, and metal arriving during the brief summer are sorted into separate stores. When the sea closes, the island governs not only its stock but the order in which states receive necessities.

## Island order

**Icebreaker Bay** is the deep harbour that does not freeze shut. **Wind Ridge** is the dry height of granaries and signal towers. **Salt Meadows** is the narrow coastal belt where small herds are kept. Skeldra’s interior is empty and storm-bound; permanent villages gather around the harbour road.

The island does not belong to the Frost Throne. Winter Granaries remains independent only by standing equally close to Ilyr-Khaz fjords’ compulsory winter preparation and foreign states’ sea supply.
