# Meridian Rivers

<p class="lore-lead">Asteron’s long rivers run beneath imperial ledgers, but each wharf keeps a rhythm no ruler’s ruler can fully measure.</p>

## Table-facing places

- **Blue Seal:** The customs wharf at a three-river junction.
- **Dream Threshold:** A quiet Kalashtar guesthouse and mediation square.
- **Glass Wing:** A small observatory-village of Vedalken flood recorders.
- **Root Bond:** A Khoravar boatwright quarter known for articulated hulls.

## Order and livelihood

A vessel is recognized first by its manifest. Night travel is lawful, but unloading requires a spoken witness from the night watch. Khoravar work in shipyards, Vedalken in records, and Kalashtar in difficult hearings.

## Pressure and hooks

The **Imperial River Office** demands uniformity while the **Independent Wharf Councils** defend local barter. Delayed seals become lost cargo, a dock strike and a de facto division of the river.

- Three captains shared one dream at Dream Threshold and now accuse one another.
- A manifest from Blue Seal cannot survive water.
- Glass Wing’s flood chart predicts a flood coming from land.
