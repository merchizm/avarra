# Copper Principalities
 <p class="lore-lead"> Seven independent principalities of Autumn Eladrin, High Elves, and Men. Loss, memory, harvest and voluntary renunciation are considered sacred. </p>

## Root-gate policy

The threshold in Copper Key Grove is not common property of the seven principalities, but a shared responsibility. If one principality seeks to open it, witnesses from the other six and the grove’s seasonal assent are required. A proposal to loosen this rule over Last Harvest debts is testing the council’s principles of memory and renunciation.
