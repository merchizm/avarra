# Republic of the Mist Islands
 <p class="lore-lead"> Island republic in the open sea, governed by trade councils and the anonymous Ninth Table. It sells identity and insurance law to outsiders; its real power lies in faceless networks, state secrets, and contract-held assassins. </p>
