# Nine Lantern Corps

 <p class="lore-lead"> The nine-city underground confederation of the people of Nimrûn, as the Svirfneblin call themselves. The Union's army is small; Its defense is based on sound magic, illusionary tunnels and controlled collapses. </p>

## Nine cities

1. First Light — confederation assembly.
2. Deep Lens — crystal optics and observation.
3. Echo Well — law and sound recordings.
4. Blue Stone — water engineering.
5. Broken Lantern — Duergar border stronghold.
6. Thin Bell — surface trade.
7. Sleeping Ore — mining and sanctuary.
8. Shadow Meter — surveyors and spies.
9. Last Glitter — The lost city on the edge of stagnation.
