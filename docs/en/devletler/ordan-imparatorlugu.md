# Ordan Empire
<p class="lore-lead">Ordan believes unmanaged nature produces famine and monsters. It offers roads, granaries and courts in exchange for taxation, soldiers and cultural alignment.</p>
<div class="lore-grid"><div class="lore-card"><h3>Succession</h3><p>General Varessa, Prince Odran and High Priest Maelor each claim the future of the empire.</p></div><div class="lore-card"><h3>Capital</h3><p>Ordanor, a planned city of censuses, public granaries and state schools.</p></div></div>
