## Root-gate policy

The passage near Moonseed is handled together with debt relief and the right of refuge in the Union. Threshold keepers place the living threshold’s hospitality promise above a commercial contract; merchant families seek to limit the advantage of learning gate news first. The seventh-year debt release also reopens old debts of reciprocity related to the gate.
