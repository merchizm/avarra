## Political fault lines

- **The Ordan Succession War:** General Varessa, Prince Odran, and High Priest Maelor struggle for the empire, with consequences across every continent.
- **The Thorn Throne Crisis:** Queen Elyra’s transformation nears completion while her successor remains unknown.
- **The Human Homeland Question:** An autonomous province, forest citizenship, and a new migration homeland compete as futures for displaced humans.
- **The Underground Inheritance War:** Kar-Dur, Kor-Dum, Black Anvil, and Nine Lanterns claim ancient dwarven cities.

## SD 83: roads, credit, and war

The power of a state depends less on the land it holds than on its ability to move grain, salt, metal, and news. Ordan’s land army depends on Nareth grain and Forty Bridges credit; the River Throne’s internal trade depends on currents; Kar-Dur and the underground cities depend on the Foundry Line; and the Mist Isles depend on secret information and faceless networks. Varkesh opens grain, payroll, and repair credit for war in one harbour. Winter Granaries holds the grain and siege-supply chain, while the Black Seal Isles maintain the intercontinental standard for military payment.

| Tension | Political and economic connection |
| --- | --- |
| Thorn Crown ↔ Ordan | Hostility over forest fortresses, grain, and the Thorn-Meridian Road. |
| Kar-Dur ↔ Nine Lanterns | Ancient vein roads, mineral inheritance, and rival maps. |
| River Throne ↔ Kar-Dur / Thorn Crown | Source waters, watershed crossings, and Three Mouths transport. |
| Lethar ↔ Frost / Copper | Dynastic diplomacy, the Star Garden Road, and northern trade. |
| Red Saar ↔ Jewel Oath | Dragon inheritance, military trade, and Odrakan roads. |

When flow slows at Namaris, grain shipments are delayed and prices in Halvek and Ordanor, debts at Forty Bridges, and salt reserves at Hal-Sur all enter crisis together. Economics is therefore not the background to politics; it is a direct field of conflict.
