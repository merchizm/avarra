# Two Dragon Crown of Saar

 <p class="lore-lead"> Dragonborn do not form a single nation in Odraka. Building on the legacy of ancient dragon domination, two rival states establish mutually exclusive narratives of history and governance. </p>

 <div class="lore-grid">
   <div class="lore-card"> <h3> Crimson Saar </h3> <p> <b> Government: </b> Dragon noble houses <br> <b> Capital: </b> Saar-Kor <br> <b> Culture: </b> Fire, honor, military service, and family memory. </p> </div>
   <div class="lore-card"> <h3> Gem Oath </h3> <p> <b> Government: </b> Gem Dragonborn monasteries and city councils <br> <b> Capital: </b> Prism <br> <b> Culture: </b> Mental discipline, memory crystals, and rejection of dragon domination. </p> </div>

## The root of competition

Red Saar believes the First Dragons created them to rule the Dragonborn and holds their ancient volcano stronghold. The Jewel Oath reads the same inheritance as a warning that no lineage has the natural right to rule over others.
