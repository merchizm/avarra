# Loxodon

<p class="lore-lead">An elephant folk who carry memory, waterworks, and a patient council tradition together.</p>

## Place in Avarra

**Ivory Reeds** is a small but open settlement in [Wind–Reed Corridor](../cografya/bolgeler/odraka-ruzgar-saz), established around Odraka delta. It is not a separate kingdom viewed from outside; it is a village or quarter node tied to neighbouring roads, markets, and common law.

## City life and diaspora

In [Sazkalp](../yerlesimler/sazkalp), Loxodon households participate in levees, water shares, and archives without being confined to a single “foreign quarter.” Someone arriving from the village can find kin, an apprenticeship, a guest bed, or a partner; city-born people maintain regular ties to Ivory Reeds.

## Living together

Loxodon are not reduced to one temperament, profession, or politics. Local agreements, access needs, and safety practices are made explicit so difference remains part of ordinary life. Their visible expertise is **levees, water shares, and archives**, but it is never anyone’s compulsory destiny.

## Adventure ties

- The partnership between Ivory Reeds and Sazkalp can be tested by a cut road or a lost record.
- A family may need mediation between local custom and city law.
- A character may belong to this record through a guild, neighbourhood, or road oath as well as ancestry.
