# Flamekin

<p class="lore-lead">A cultural lineage within fire-genasi kinships that treats hearth care and safe heat as public responsibility.</p>

## Place in Avarra

**Ember Roof** is a small but open settlement in [Rift Lakes and Harbours](../cografya/bolgeler/odraka-yarik-limanlari), established around Odraka volcanic shores. It is not a separate kingdom viewed from outside; it is a village or quarter node tied to neighbouring roads, markets, and common law.

## City life and diaspora

In [Saar-Kor](../yerlesimler/saar-kor), Flamekin households participate in ovens, ceramics, and safe-heat infrastructure without being confined to a single “foreign quarter.” Someone arriving from the village can find kin, an apprenticeship, a guest bed, or a partner; city-born people maintain regular ties to Ember Roof.

## Living together

Flamekin are not reduced to one temperament, profession, or politics. Local agreements, access needs, and safety practices are made explicit so difference remains part of ordinary life. Their visible expertise is **ovens, ceramics, and safe-heat infrastructure**, but it is never anyone’s compulsory destiny.

## Adventure ties

- The partnership between Ember Roof and Saar-Kor can be tested by a cut road or a lost record.
- A family may need mediation between local custom and city law.
- A character may belong to this record through a guild, neighbourhood, or road oath as well as ancestry.
