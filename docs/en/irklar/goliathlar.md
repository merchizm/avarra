# Goliaths

<p class="lore-lead">A high-mountain people of the Ilyr-Khaz ice roads, who place passage rights and shared burdens above blood superiority.</p>

Every journey is a shared responsibility: even the strongest traveler shares the fate of the slowest in a storm. Their road wardens keep the northern passes open as both sacred and economic work.
