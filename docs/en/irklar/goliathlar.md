# Goliaths
 <p class="lore-lead"> High mountain folk on the Ilyr-Khaz ice roads, who prioritize right of way and burden sharing over lineage. </p>
 <p> Goliath communities consider every journey a mutual responsibility; Even the strongest traveler suffers the same fate as the slowest person in a storm. The iceway guardians consider it both an economic and sacred duty to keep the northern passages open. </p>

## Canonical record

 <p> Goliaths live in Ilyr-Khaz and the high mountains. There are communities whose mountains accept the bodies of dead giants or still dormant wills. </p>
 <p> Competition is a method of preparing the group for difficult conditions rather than individual superiority. It is considered valuable not to abandon someone because of weakness, but to honestly accept the burden that the group cannot bear. The Goliaths who live in the Frost Palace are affected by the ice protection law. </p>
