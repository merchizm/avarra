# Giants and Fomorians
 <p class="lore-lead"> Ancient great peoples, unknown whether they were a single civilization or rival dynasties, leaving traces from ice roads to sunken pillars. </p>
 <p> Fomorian shoots, giant ruins, and ancient gateways are both resources and threats to modern states. No present-day community has an undisputed right to this heritage. </p>

## Fomorians and Fey exile

Some traditions describe parts of the Fomorian lineages as giants exiled from Fey or altered by Fey oaths; this is not one verified origin. Fomorians had no single side or army in the Veil Wars. A present Fomorian house’s tie to Rootbeyond may be an old debt, refused hospitality, or only a rumour imposed on it by others.

## Canonical record

 <p> Stone, Ice, Fire and Cloud Giants are considered to have built some of the first large structures. Today's giant communities are the fragmented descendants of these dynasties. </p>
 <p> Fomorians are a race of giants exiled from the Fey world or corrupted by its pacts. Not every Fomorian adheres to the same malevolent culture; Some want to regain their old forms. </p>
