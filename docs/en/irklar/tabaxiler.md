# Tabaxies
 <p class="lore-lead"> Families in rainforest cities who accept knowledge, stories, and rare objects as wealth. </p>

## Canonical record

 <p> Rain Claw Tabaxi are worth as much story, method, and the right question as rare items. Family archives preserve oral narratives together with material collections. </p>
 <p> Their curiosity is not irresponsible; The information collected is expected to return to the community. The Market of Nine Souls is one of the continent's largest centers of knowledge and rare goods. </p>
