# Giff

<p class="lore-lead">A people who distinguish discipline from domination, skilled in heavy freight, road safety, and instruction.</p>

## Place in Avarra

**Three Posts** is a small but open settlement in [Asteron’s Northern Passes](../cografya/bolgeler/asteron-kuzey-gecitleri), established around Asteron northern passes. It is not a separate kingdom viewed from outside; it is a village or quarter node tied to neighbouring roads, markets, and common law.

## City life and diaspora

In [Saar-Kor](../yerlesimler/saar-kor), Giff households participate in logistics, safety training, and crane work without being confined to a single “foreign quarter.” Someone arriving from the village can find kin, an apprenticeship, a guest bed, or a partner; city-born people maintain regular ties to Three Posts.

## Living together

Giff are not reduced to one temperament, profession, or politics. Local agreements, access needs, and safety practices are made explicit so difference remains part of ordinary life. Their visible expertise is **logistics, safety training, and crane work**, but it is never anyone’s compulsory destiny.

## Adventure ties

- The partnership between Three Posts and Saar-Kor can be tested by a cut road or a lost record.
- A family may need mediation between local custom and city law.
- A character may belong to this record through a guild, neighbourhood, or road oath as well as ancestry.
