# kenkular
 <p class="lore-lead"> Nomadic news and craft people who carry the sounds they collect from the world rather than their own songs. </p>

## Canonical record

 <p> Kenkus work as live recorders, messengers, performers, and contract witnesses in the cities of Imitation Harbor and Rain Claw. Just because they can repeat other people's voices doesn't mean they don't have their own thoughts. </p>
 <p> The voice quoted by a Kenku court witness is strong evidence; This makes them both respected and a target. Their originality is seen in their mastery of sound selection, context and combination. </p>
