# Leonin

<p class="lore-lead">A lion folk who interpret herd behaviour not as property but as shared responsibility and road knowledge.</p>

## Place in Avarra

**Lion Threshold** is a small but open settlement in [Wind–Reed Corridor](../cografya/bolgeler/odraka-ruzgar-saz), established around Odraka savanna. It is not a separate kingdom viewed from outside; it is a village or quarter node tied to neighbouring roads, markets, and common law.

## City life and diaspora

In [Saar-Kor](../yerlesimler/saar-kor), Leonin households participate in tracking, route mediation, and wardenship without being confined to a single “foreign quarter.” Someone arriving from the village can find kin, an apprenticeship, a guest bed, or a partner; city-born people maintain regular ties to Lion Threshold.

## Living together

Leonin are not reduced to one temperament, profession, or politics. Local agreements, access needs, and safety practices are made explicit so difference remains part of ordinary life. Their visible expertise is **tracking, route mediation, and wardenship**, but it is never anyone’s compulsory destiny.

## Adventure ties

- The partnership between Lion Threshold and Saar-Kor can be tested by a cut road or a lost record.
- A family may need mediation between local custom and city law.
- A character may belong to this record through a guild, neighbourhood, or road oath as well as ancestry.
