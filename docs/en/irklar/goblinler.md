# Goblins
 <p class="lore-lead"> Goblins draw strength from the unseen business of cities rather than a great empire: night markets, fungicides, waste sorting, canal maintenance, and funeral services are united in their guild culture. </p>

## A disputed Fey heritage

Some Goblin guilds tell stories reaching back to the First Fey; others dismiss them as an old label invented by patrons. Fey connection is therefore not a compulsory key to Goblin origin. One guild may bargain with a court or work around a gate, without that relationship extending to all Goblins.

## Canonical record

 <p> Goblin power comes not from a great nation, but from the unseen workings of cities. They establish intercontinental guilds in the field of night markets, fungicides, scrap sorting, canal maintenance and funeral services. </p>
 <p> Goblin workers in the Salt Mines request citizenship. City Goblins have a low status in law, despite being essential to the survival of most states. Some traditions associate them with the First Fey peoples. </p>
