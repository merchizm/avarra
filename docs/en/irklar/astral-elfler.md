# Astral Elves

<p class="lore-lead">Elven communities reconciling traditions of sky travel with Avarra’s seasons.</p>

## Place in Avarra

**Star Rest** is a small but open settlement in [Rift Lakes and Harbours](../cografya/bolgeler/odraka-yarik-limanlari), established around Odraka rift lakes. It is not a separate kingdom viewed from outside; it is a village or quarter node tied to neighbouring roads, markets, and common law.

## City life and diaspora

In [Astereth](../yerlesimler/astereth), Astral Elves households participate in sky-reading, long routes, and star archives without being confined to a single “foreign quarter.” Someone arriving from the village can find kin, an apprenticeship, a guest bed, or a partner; city-born people maintain regular ties to Star Rest.

## Living together

Astral Elves are not reduced to one temperament, profession, or politics. Local agreements, access needs, and safety practices are made explicit so difference remains part of ordinary life. Their visible expertise is **sky-reading, long routes, and star archives**, but it is never anyone’s compulsory destiny.

## Adventure ties

- The partnership between Star Rest and Astereth can be tested by a cut road or a lost record.
- A family may need mediation between local custom and city law.
- A character may belong to this record through a guild, neighbourhood, or road oath as well as ancestry.
