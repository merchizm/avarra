# Plasmoids

<p class="lore-lead">A people who change bodily form for personal expression and practical adaptation, defending care rights alongside citizenship.</p>

## Place in Avarra

**Gel Caravanserai** is a small but open settlement in [Pale Basin and Salt Roads](../cografya/bolgeler/nareth-soluk-canak), established around Nareth salt basin. It is not a separate kingdom viewed from outside; it is a village or quarter node tied to neighbouring roads, markets, and common law.

## City life and diaspora

In [Namaris](../yerlesimler/namaris), Plasmoids households participate in caravan pools and delicate freight without being confined to a single “foreign quarter.” Someone arriving from the village can find kin, an apprenticeship, a guest bed, or a partner; city-born people maintain regular ties to Gel Caravanserai.

## Living together

Plasmoids are not reduced to one temperament, profession, or politics. Local agreements, access needs, and safety practices are made explicit so difference remains part of ordinary life. Their visible expertise is **caravan pools and delicate freight**, but it is never anyone’s compulsory destiny.

## Adventure ties

- The partnership between Gel Caravanserai and Namaris can be tested by a cut road or a lost record.
- A family may need mediation between local custom and city law.
- A character may belong to this record through a guild, neighbourhood, or road oath as well as ancestry.
