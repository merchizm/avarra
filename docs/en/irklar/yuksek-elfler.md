# High Elves
 <p class="lore-lead"> Lethar The Elven branch of the Star Kingdom associated with its observatories, conduits, and schools of magic. </p>

## Canonical record

 <p> The most powerful culture of the High Elves is the Lethar; however, distinct communities exist in the Copper Principalities, Ordan cities, and independent academies. </p>
 <p> Lethar culture celebrates the star, education, planned gardens, and long-term politics. Noble families regard the social order as a reflection of the heavenly order. Low Star folk and city Gnomes bear the financial burden of this grace. </p>
