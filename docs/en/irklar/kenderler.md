# Kender

<p class="lore-lead">A travelling people known for news and lost-object networks who have learned to distinguish curiosity from consent.</p>

## Place in Avarra

**Bell Stop** is a small but open settlement in [Wind–Reed Corridor](../cografya/bolgeler/odraka-ruzgar-saz), established around Odraka migration roads. It is not a separate kingdom viewed from outside; it is a village or quarter node tied to neighbouring roads, markets, and common law.

## City life and diaspora

In [Dokuz Can Pazarı](../yerlesimler/dokuz-can-pazari), Kender households participate in news networks, finding the lost, and hospitality without being confined to a single “foreign quarter.” Someone arriving from the village can find kin, an apprenticeship, a guest bed, or a partner; city-born people maintain regular ties to Bell Stop.

## Living together

Kender are not reduced to one temperament, profession, or politics. Local agreements, access needs, and safety practices are made explicit so difference remains part of ordinary life. Their visible expertise is **news networks, finding the lost, and hospitality**, but it is never anyone’s compulsory destiny.

## Adventure ties

- The partnership between Bell Stop and Dokuz Can Pazarı can be tested by a cut road or a lost record.
- A family may need mediation between local custom and city law.
- A character may belong to this record through a guild, neighbourhood, or road oath as well as ancestry.
