# Kalamer

<p class="lore-lead">A merfolk kinship adapted to shorelines that change shape with the tide, living on land and in shallows.</p>

## Place in Avarra

**Walking Coral** is a small but open settlement in [Mist Chain and Deep Current](../cografya/bolgeler/sis-zinciri), established around Mist Chain islands. It is not a separate kingdom viewed from outside; it is a village or quarter node tied to neighbouring roads, markets, and common law.

## City life and diaspora

In [Mavi Taç](../yerlesimler/mavi-tac), Kalamer households participate in shoal piloting and tidal farming without being confined to a single “foreign quarter.” Someone arriving from the village can find kin, an apprenticeship, a guest bed, or a partner; city-born people maintain regular ties to Walking Coral.

## Living together

Kalamer are not reduced to one temperament, profession, or politics. Local agreements, access needs, and safety practices are made explicit so difference remains part of ordinary life. Their visible expertise is **shoal piloting and tidal farming**, but it is never anyone’s compulsory destiny.

## Adventure ties

- The partnership between Walking Coral and Mavi Taç can be tested by a cut road or a lost record.
- A family may need mediation between local custom and city law.
- A character may belong to this record through a guild, neighbourhood, or road oath as well as ancestry.
