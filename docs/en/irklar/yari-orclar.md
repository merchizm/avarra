# Half-Orcs

<p class="lore-lead">Half-Orc is neither one culture nor one political side, but a lineage condition found among kinships of the Red Earth, border cities, and many human communities.</p>

They are often full members of their own neighbourhoods, clans, and guilds; border mediation is a skill, not their inevitable role.
