# Half-Orcs

 <p class="lore-lead"> Half-Orc is not a single culture or political faction; The Red Land is a lineage seen in kinships between border cities and different human communities. </p>

## Canonical record

 <p> Orcs are not the only nomadic marauders in Avarra. The Red Earth Kingdom is a powerful state with a kingdom of controlled fire, steppe agriculture, and selective warfare. The Endless Way Orc communities participate in Centaur migration law. </p>
 <p> Half-Orc identity is not a single intermediate category. Mixed communities formed with humans, Veyran or other humanoid families have different cultures. In the Red Land, half-Orcs are not considered a separate and inferior lineage. </p>
