# Eladrin

<p class="lore-lead">Eladrin are an ancient people who have learned not to hide their emotions, but to live them as seasons. An Eladrin's face, voice, and even the air around them can change with the season that rules within their soul.</p>

<div class="lore-callout">“To know an Eladrin, do not watch their face; listen to what the wind says while you stand beside them.” <br>— <em>From the notes of the traveller Sera Venn</em></div>

## Rootbeyond and court ties

Eladrin are not the only people who can enter Rootbeyond or descend from it, but they are among the most visible carriers of the free courts’ order of stories and oaths. An Eladrin’s season does not determine court membership: a spring mediator and a winter mourner may share a court. Those who pass a gate remain subject to the Echo-Root’s three terms; even in the proper season, passage is unreliable without a living threshold and words recognised on the other side.

## Seasonal faces

Eladrin are not bound to one temperament. When their inner world changes profoundly, it appears in physical details and in the character of their magic. These states are not a classification, but a cycle an Eladrin may travel many times in a lifetime.

<div class="lore-grid">
  <div class="lore-card"><h3>Spring</h3><p>Curiosity, renewal, and forgiveness. Its flowers are remembered not in pale green, but in dawn-pink and young-gold tones.</p></div>
  <div class="lore-card"><h3>Summer</h3><p>Joy, courage, and burning loyalty. Their words are short, their laughter easy, and their devotion to what they protect is immense.</p></div>
  <div class="lore-card"><h3>Autumn</h3><p>Generosity, storytelling, and gravity. Those who remember forgotten names are often in this season.</p></div>
  <div class="lore-card"><h3>Winter</h3><p>Stillness, grief, and sharp clarity. Their coldness is not cruelty; it is the way they give form to feeling.</p></div>
</div>

## Society and custom

Eladrin communities are organized less around cities than around **courts** and **houses**. A court need not contain those who share a season; it is more often defined by a common oath, an old debt, or a passage under protection. Decisions are commonly made not through written statute, but by reference to a story already told.

### Names and oaths

An Eladrin's birth name is used only by those close to them. Chosen travelling names describe the story a person carries: **One Who Came After Ashes**, **One Who Crossed Three Doors**, or **Daughter of the Silent Lake**. Breaking a sworn oath is not merely a loss of reputation; it can leave a lasting fracture in one's seasonal harmony.

### Relations with the human world

Short-lived peoples often see Eladrin as either unreachable sages or unreliable changelings. Their deepest difficulty is the speed of human generations: within the span of a single friendship, they may see a town rise, be forgotten, and be founded again.

## Canonical record

<p>An Eladrin's season is more than body colour or mood. It is an identity tied to fey currents, faith, and political community. An Eladrin can rarely change seasons; some states treat this as conversion, others as the abandonment of citizenship.</p>
