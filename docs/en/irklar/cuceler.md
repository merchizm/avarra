# Dwarves

<p class="lore-lead">Avarran dwarves do not live beneath a single crown. Kar-Dur’s hearth councils, the oligarchs of the Salt Hearths, and rival claims over the underground inheritance interpret the memory of stone in different ways.</p>

## Hearth law

The crown governs foreign war and roads, but has limited direct authority over mines. The Sworn resist reopening ancient roads, while the Openers seek cooperation with Ordan.

## Canonical record

<p>Kar-Dur Mountain Dwarves live around stone records, oaths, and hearth law. A family name carries not only inheritance, but unfinished duty and debt.</p>
<p>Crown Hearths are military-aristocratic, Deep Masters economic and technical, and Surface Dwarves cosmopolitan cultures. Kar-Dur is not the state of all dwarves.</p>
