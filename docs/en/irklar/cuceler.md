## Quarry law

The crown rules foreign war and roads; It has limited direct authority over mines. While the Sworn Ones oppose the opening of the ancient roads, the Openers seek cooperation with Ordan.

## Canonical record

 <p> Kar-Dur Mountain Dwarves live around stone records, oaths, and the law of the hearth. A surname carries not only inheritance but also unfinished duties and debts. </p>
 <p> The Crown Forges are military-aristocratic, the Deep Masters are economic-technical, and the Surface Dwarves are cosmopolitan cultures. Kar-Dur is not the state of all dwarves. </p>
