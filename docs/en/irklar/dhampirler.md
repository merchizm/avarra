# Dhampirs
 <p class="lore-lead"> Night travelers trying to balance their extraordinary needs for blood, prey, and hunger with their own laws. </p>

## Canonical record

 <p> Dhampirs can appear as a result of a blood curse, parasite, ancient medical process, vampiric contact, or Stasis infection. They are not a single lineage or nation. </p>
 <p> In the Copper Principalities, some Dhampir families hold respected positions in mourning, funerals, and custody of dangerous corpses. They may be hunted or recorded in other states. </p>
