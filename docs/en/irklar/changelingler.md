# Changelings


## D&D reference

Changelings are shape-shifting humanoids. In default use, they can resemble other humanoids in appearance and voice, but cannot copy clothing or items. It is therefore a powerful narrative foundation for the spy, diplomat, fake identity expert, or multi-identity city citizen; Avarra's debt law gives this ability direct political consequences.

## Canonical record

 <p> Changelings live most densely on the Mist Islands. They are not a single secret people, but communities of citizens who contractually govern multiple legal identities. </p>
 <p> In succession law, the debts and crimes of each identity can be recorded separately; But some serious crimes committed by the body apply to all identities. Impersonating a face without permission is identity theft, and wearing the face of a dead person is a mourning ritual in some families. </p>
