# Thornbloods


## D&D reference

The Thorns are a people native to Avarra; In default D&D, the closest game frameworks are Firbolg, Satyr, and plant-fey themed creatures. With features such as mechanical durability, thorny close defense, vegetative concealment or short-term rooting; In the narrative, fey can be used as humanoids.

## Canonical record

 <p> Thornbloods are ancient humanoids that do not have animal characteristics, but have shells, leaves, woody horns and thorn protrusions. They form the bulk of the Crown of Thorns army. </p>
 <p> Their body growth may vary with environment, age and task rather than family. Cutting off horns or branches signifies mourning, punishment, or military discipline. The Hornless Army is an equal citizenship movement against the imposed military caste identity. </p>
