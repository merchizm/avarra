# Humans

<p class="lore-lead">No internationally recognised human state remains in Nareth. The legacy of the old five kingdoms endures through human communities divided between forest refuges and Ordan citizenship.</p>

## Five exile dynasties

Red Deer seeks a war of reclamation; Broken Tower seeks autonomy within Ordan; White Hearth defends citizenship with the Thorn Crown; Black Reed runs the de facto safety network. The name and heraldry of the Nameless Dynasty were forgotten in the Stagnation.

## Canonical record

<p>Humans are not Avarra’s new people. They have lived in Nareth and Asteron since the Age of First Awakenings. The end of Nareth’s five kingdoms did not unite humans into one culture: exile, imperial citizenship, and arguments over a new homeland created different identities.</p>

## Major cultures

<div class="lore-grid"><div class="lore-card"><h3>Old Crowned</h3><p>Maintain the lost dynasties, heraldry, and legal order.</p></div><div class="lore-card"><h3>Woodborn</h3><p>Born in the forests of the Thorn Crown, they have adopted mobile settlement and multilingual life.</p></div><div class="lore-card"><h3>Road Hearths</h3><p>Mobile communities living along news, migration, and escape routes.</p></div><div class="lore-card"><h3>Imperial Humans</h3><p>Develop different forms of accommodation, autonomy, or resistance under Ordan citizenship.</p></div><div class="lore-card"><h3>Odrakan Humans</h3><p>Live within port, delta, steppe, and dragon states while remaining tied to local cultures.</p></div></div>

<p class="lore-callout">The central conflict of human identity is between rebuilding past kingdoms and becoming a new people where one lives.</p>
