# people

 <p class="lore-lead"> There are no internationally recognized human states left in Nareth. The legacy of the ancient five kingdoms lives on, with human communities divided between forest refuges and Ordan citizenship. </p>



## Five exiled dynasties

Red Deer wants a war of retrieval; The Broken Tower seeks autonomy within the Horde; The White Hearth defends citizenship with the Crown of Thorns; The Black Reed runs the de facto safety net. The name and crest of the Nameless Dynasty were forgotten in the Stagnation.

## Canonical record

 <p> The humans are not the new people of Avarra. They have been found in Nareth and Seyvar since the Age of First Awakenings. The end of the five kingdoms of Nareth did not unite people into a single culture; Discussions of exile, imperial citizenship and new homeland created different identities. </p>
