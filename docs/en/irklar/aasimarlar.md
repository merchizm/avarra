# Aasimars
 <p class="lore-lead"> Being Aasimar is not a separate nationality. An Aasimar can be born as an elf, dwarf, human, or Veyran; States may consider them as prophets, dangers, instruments of inheritance or state property. </p>

## Canonical record

 <p> Being Aasimar is not a separate nationality. They can be born with a divine or cosmic mark into a Human, Elf, Dwarf, Veyran, or other people. </p>
 <p> Lethar trains them at Silent Meridian; Ordan records them; some people consider them a sign of lost kingdoms. It is not certain that Aasimars were chosen by gods, and some reject the mark’s meaning. Outside Astereth, seeing even one or two Aasimars in an ordinary city is unusual: families often try to reach Lethar, hide, or evade foreign registrars when a mark appears. </p>
