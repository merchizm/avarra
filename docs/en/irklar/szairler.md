# Szaïr

<p class="lore-lead">The Szaïr are urban Qasyrrans who carry wealth on the body as well as in the vault, making debt, house ties, and contract into visible signs.</p>

## Place in Avarra

Szaïr houses are the most visible face of Qasyrra’s dye, spice, resin, and oil trade. Their presence in ports does not represent the empire’s interior; many foreigners mistake the Qasyrra they know through Szaïr intermediaries for the whole country.

## Adventure ties

- A string of coins on a family veil shows that a contract thought closed is still in force.
- A port guild accuses a Szaïr trader of forging a debt record; the real document is in a house archive opened only at night.
