# Thri-kreen

<p class="lore-lead">An insect folk known for multi-handed work and for negotiating compatible rests and watches.</p>

## Place in Avarra

**Six-Leg Market** is a small but open settlement in [Wind–Reed Corridor](../cografya/bolgeler/odraka-ruzgar-saz), established around Odraka savanna. It is not a separate kingdom viewed from outside; it is a village or quarter node tied to neighbouring roads, markets, and common law.

## City life and diaspora

In [Mazaros](../yerlesimler/mazaros), Thri-kreen households participate in light gear, watch design, and dry-route knowledge without being confined to a single “foreign quarter.” Someone arriving from the village can find kin, an apprenticeship, a guest bed, or a partner; city-born people maintain regular ties to Six-Leg Market.

## Living together

Thri-kreen are not reduced to one temperament, profession, or politics. Local agreements, access needs, and safety practices are made explicit so difference remains part of ordinary life. Their visible expertise is **light gear, watch design, and dry-route knowledge**, but it is never anyone’s compulsory destiny.

## Adventure ties

- The partnership between Six-Leg Market and Mazaros can be tested by a cut road or a lost record.
- A family may need mediation between local custom and city law.
- A character may belong to this record through a guild, neighbourhood, or road oath as well as ancestry.
