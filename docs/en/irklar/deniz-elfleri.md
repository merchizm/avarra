# Sea Elves
 <p class="lore-lead"> The Elven branch of the Coral Palaces, living in coastal and shallow sea cities, defending water rights up to the river mouths. </p>

## Canonical record

 <p> Sea Elves live in the Coral Palaces, River Throne, and independent coastal communities. They do not accept the coastline as a natural political boundary; They consider tidal areas and estuaries as part of the common water geography. </p>
 <p> Families can have homes of varying depths. Port construction and waste dumping by surface states are the biggest source of diplomatic conflict. </p>
