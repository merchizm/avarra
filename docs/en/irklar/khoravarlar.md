# Khoravar

<p class="lore-lead">A people living at intersections of Elven and human kinship without subscribing to a single story of purity.</p>

## Place in Avarra

**Root Bond** is a small but open settlement in [Meridian Rivers](../cografya/bolgeler/asteron-meridyen-nehirleri), established around Asteron shipyards. It is not a separate kingdom viewed from outside; it is a village or quarter node tied to neighbouring roads, markets, and common law.

## City life and diaspora

In [Ordanor](../yerlesimler/ordanor), Khoravar households participate in boat carpentry and inheritance mediation without being confined to a single “foreign quarter.” Someone arriving from the village can find kin, an apprenticeship, a guest bed, or a partner; city-born people maintain regular ties to Root Bond.

## Living together

Khoravar are not reduced to one temperament, profession, or politics. Local agreements, access needs, and safety practices are made explicit so difference remains part of ordinary life. Their visible expertise is **boat carpentry and inheritance mediation**, but it is never anyone’s compulsory destiny.

## Adventure ties

- The partnership between Root Bond and Ordanor can be tested by a cut road or a lost record.
- A family may need mediation between local custom and city law.
- A character may belong to this record through a guild, neighbourhood, or road oath as well as ancestry.
