# Hobgoblins
 <p class="lore-lead"> Military citizens who value order, trained troops, and earned responsibility over blood ties. </p>

## Canonical record

 <p> Not all Hobgoblins are soldiers. Some communities within Külordu and Ordan developed a professional military culture. Logistics, engineering, medicine and discipline are as valuable as warriorship. </p>
 <p> Odraka colonies such as Black Rain are intermixed with local cultures. The Külordu Mark sells soldiers to Ordan, but maintains its independence. </p>
