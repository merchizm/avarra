# Veyrans

 <p class="lore-lead"> Veyrans are a completely humanoid-looking people, known for their double-ringed irises and fine natural lines on their hands. Just because they are the founding people of Ordan does not mean that all Veyran support the empire. </p>



## Cultural distinction

Meridian Veyrans consider recording and measurement sacred. Border Veyrans are intertwined with local cultures; The unlined rise in the examination bureaucracy against the old nobility laws; Anti-Establishmentists, on the other hand, argue that the empire is killing nature.

## Canonical record

 <p> Veyrans have a completely humanoid appearance. Their skin may be grey, copper, dark olive or pale bronze. Two intertwined iris rings in his eyes; They have fine natural lines on their neck and hands. </p>
 <p> Veyrans founded Ordan, but not all Veyrans are pro-imperial. </p>

## Major cultures

 <div class="lore-grid"> <div class="lore-card"> <h3> Meridian Veyrans </h3> <p> Capital culture that considers written law, examination and measurement as the basis of civilization. </p> </div> <div class="lore-card"> <h3> Border Veyrans </h3> <p> Born in conquered regions; communities shaped by local languages and mixed families. </p> </div> <div class="lore-card"> <h3> Unlined </h3> <p> Veyrans whose lineage is seen as weak and who are excluded from the old nobility but can rise in the bureaucracy. </p> </div> <div class="lore-card"> <h3> Anti-Establishment </h3> <p> Rural and religious movements that argue that the recording of the world is suffocating Avarra. </p> </div> </div>

 <p class="lore-callout"> Families consider their children's education and exam success as mutual dignity. Losing documents is not only an administrative problem, it is a loss of social identity. </p>
