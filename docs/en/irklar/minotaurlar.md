# Minotaurs

 <p class="lore-lead"> Minotaurs consider the labyrinth not a prison, but a sacred symbol that shows that the right decision is not just a single straight path. The law, maritime and city plan of the Labyrinth Ports are shaped by this understanding. </p>

## Canonical record

 <p> Minotaurs of the Labyrinth Ports emphasize navigation, mathematics, law, and indirect speech. The labyrinth is not a prison, but a sacred symbol that shows that there is no single right path. </p>
 <p> Responding directly to a disagreement can be considered impulsive decision-making. City roads serve as both defense and memory; each turn is associated with an old case or sea expedition. </p>
