# Grungs
 <p class="lore-lead"> Small amphibian people organized by color and task order in the pond cities of the rainforest. </p>

## Canonical record

 <p> Grungs establish independent reed villages, healing communities, and mercenary scout corps in the Marsh Dragon kingdoms. Body color was a strict marker of class in some ancient societies; Quiet Delta reformers seek to abolish this order. </p>
 <p> The secretions from their skin are used in the production of medicines, poisons and ceremonial dyes. Taking this item without permission is considered physical assault. </p>
