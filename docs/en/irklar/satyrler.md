# Satyrs
 <p class="lore-lead"> Horned wanderers who guard the bonds, music, and law of hospitality around the Fey gateways. </p>

## Canonical record

 <p> Satyrs live in Altıngüneş, around Thorn Crown and in independent vineyard villages. Festival cultures are not frivolity, but a controlled discharge of debts, conflicts and social pressure. </p>
 <p> Music can be a tool of legal testimony, sarcasm a tool of political criticism. Silencing a Satyr community means removing public scrutiny, not entertainment. </p>
