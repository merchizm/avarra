# Grimlocks
 <p class="lore-lead"> Depth communities that live by reading the vibration of stones, air and steps rather than sight. </p>
 <p> In Grimlock cultures, silence is not absence; It is a detailed language that the ground tells. Surface sources often falsely dehumanize them; whereas knowledge of passages, prey, and vibrations is essential to the survival of many underground cities. </p>

## Canonical record

 <p> Grimlock cities are designed not by visual cues but by ground texture, airflow, and echo. Their blindness is not accepted as a deficiency but as a different perception of space. </p>
 <p> Loud voices are considered aggression. Wall reliefs are means of writing and vibration wells are means of communication. All underground states need Grimlocks because of their knowledge of earthquakes. </p>
