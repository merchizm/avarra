# Gnomes

<p class="lore-lead">Gnomish communities are not a single guild of inventors: observatories, small gardens, travelling repair hearths, and deep workshops share knowledge across Avarra.</p>

## Workshop law

The value of a device belongs not only to its owner but to those who repair and safely use it. Maintenance rights therefore matter as much as ownership, especially in Autognome citizenship debates.
