# Gnomes

 <p class="lore-lead"> Gnome societies are not a single guild of invention: they live by sharing knowledge among observatories, small gardens, mobile forges, and deep workshops. </p>

## Canonical record

 <p> Gnomes are not a monoculture. </p>
 <p> - <b> Forest Gnomes: </b> Underbranch paths, seed archives, and secret communications. - <b> Rock Gnomes: </b> Kar-Dur engineering and independent workshops. - <b> City Gnomes: </b> Astereth’s Nine Canals, star mirrors, observatory clocks, and terrace infrastructure; Lethar treats them as highly paid, indispensable specialists. City Gnomes elsewhere are usually on short duty, guild exchange, or an extension of Lethar training. - <b> Rainforest Gnomes: </b> Map, paint, medicine and tree architecture. </p>
 <p> Curiosity is valued among the Gnomes, but each culture evaluates knowledge differently: The Lower Branch stores, From there records, Forty Bridges sells. </p>
