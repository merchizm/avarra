# Ossai

<p class="lore-lead">The Ossai carry Qasyrra’s river, court, and monumental-state face most visibly, turning uncovered faces and solar headdresses into a ceremonial language.</p>

## Place in Avarra

Ossai diplomats and officials appear often in foreign records; that visibility does not make other Qasyrrans less important. The empire they represent abroad is continually remade within by water houses, castes, and distinct peoples.

## Adventure ties

- A mark on a solar headdress records not a diplomatic gift but an old river right.
- An Ossai official is caught between a palace order and the survival obligation of their water house.
