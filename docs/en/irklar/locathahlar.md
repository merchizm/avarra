# Locathahs
 <p class="lore-lead"> Fish-human communities living in the delta temples and water districts of the River Throne. </p>

## Canonical record

 <p> Locathahs are affiliated with different cultures in the River Throne, Tideless Lake, and the delta cities. It is not a single stateless fish nation. </p>
 <p> They maintain their social ties through pools of water, scent, and shared swimming rhythms. Staying away from water for long periods of time creates cultural as well as physical isolation. </p>
