# Worgs

<p class="lore-lead">Speaking Worg communities partner with other underground households for freedom of movement and safe work.</p>

## Place in Avarra

**Blind Hearth** is a small but open settlement in [Hollow Ring and Foundry Line](../cografya/bolgeler/oyuk-dokum-hatti), established around the Avarra underground. It is not a separate kingdom viewed from outside; it is a village or quarter node tied to neighbouring roads, markets, and common law.

## City life and diaspora

In [Kara Örs](../yerlesimler/kara-ors), Worgs households participate in tracking, rescue, and narrow-pass logistics without being confined to a single “foreign quarter.” Someone arriving from the village can find kin, an apprenticeship, a guest bed, or a partner; city-born people maintain regular ties to Blind Hearth.

## Living together

Worgs are not reduced to one temperament, profession, or politics. Local agreements, access needs, and safety practices are made explicit so difference remains part of ordinary life. Their visible expertise is **tracking, rescue, and narrow-pass logistics**, but it is never anyone’s compulsory destiny.

## Adventure ties

- The partnership between Blind Hearth and Kara Örs can be tested by a cut road or a lost record.
- A family may need mediation between local custom and city law.
- A character may belong to this record through a guild, neighbourhood, or road oath as well as ancestry.
