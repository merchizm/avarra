# Sahuagins
 <p class="lore-lead"> Warlike confederacy people who rule the deep-sea hunting grounds. </p>

## Canonical record

 <p> The Sahuagin Red Rift Confederacy are warrior states that rule the deep-sea hunting grounds. There are complex justice systems that use the scent of blood as legal evidence. </p>
 <p> Hunting land rights, migration of sea creatures and spoils of war are regulated in confederation congresses. Constant war with the Tritons does not make all Sahuagin reckless marauders. </p>
