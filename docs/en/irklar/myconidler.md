# Myconids


## D&D reference

Myconids are mushroom-like, sentient vegetative entities that communicate via telepathic spores. A sports circle can provide shared sharing of emotions and memories. In a D&D adventure, it's appropriate to use them as neutral archivists, healers, underworld emissaries, or victims of a contagious spore threat.

## Canonical record

 <p> The city of Myconid is less a network of buildings than a network of collective memory. The sports garden functions simultaneously as an embassy, archive and living body. </p>
