# Autognomes
 <p class="lore-lead"> Artificial little people left behind by the gnome workshops, seeking citizenship between repair and their own will. </p>

## Canonical record

 <p> Autognomes are small artificial folk who mostly awaken in old Gnome workshops. The Tick-Tock Garden is a self-repairing workshop and Autognom settlement. </p>
 <p> They have different crafting traditions than Warforged. Changing a body part is not considered a loss of identity; The memory core, chosen name, and maintained relationships are considered the foundation of the self. </p>
