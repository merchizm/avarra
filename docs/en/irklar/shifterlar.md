# Shifters
 <p class="lore-lead"> Shifters are not lycanthropes. In northern forests and mountain borders, they establish cultures associated with the seasonal behavior of game animals rather than the lunar cycle. </p>

## Canonical record

 <p> Shifters live in boreal forests and mountain borders. They are not lycanthropes; They carry old body legacies that are affected by the seasonal behavior of game animals. </p>
 <p> Wolf Bowl, Bear Threshold, and Red Trace are different hunting cultures. Animal characteristics do not necessarily determine their role in society. There are communities that hide their identities due to fear of lycanthropes from foreign states. </p>
