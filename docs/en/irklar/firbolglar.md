# Firbolgs


## D&D reference

Firbolgs are referred to in D&D as giant-related humanoids of fey origin. It is common for them to be calm, community-oriented, and nature-protective characters. Nature-fey themed abilities such as cloaking, animal/plant communication, brief invisibility, or magic perception are good starting points when turning this entry into a playable character or NPC.

## Canonical record

 <p> Firbolgs live in the Root groves of the Crown of Thorns, boreal forests, and freestanding sanctuaries. Rather than a biological household, the family is a community that shares responsibility for the same grove. </p>
 <p> The dead are delivered to mushroom gardens. It is a serious crime to disrupt the animal migration, water system or grave memory of an area. Just because they look calm doesn't mean they are passive. </p>
