# Derro
 <p class="lore-lead"> Scattered underground peoples living around broken dreams, sealed memories, and strange crafts. </p>
 <p> Derro communities do not necessarily share the same history; Some consider lost memories as sacred relics, while others consider them as dangerous cargo. Seal dreamers try to interpret the signs on ancient structures in sleep. </p>

## Canonical record

 <p> The Derro are scattered underground societies bearing traces of ancient psionic civilizations and Mind Flayer colonies. They do not have a common state. </p>
 <p> They are considered untrustworthy by other peoples due to their psionic dreams and fragmented ancestral memories. Some Derro communities view these dreams as illness, others as the legacy of a lost civilization. </p>
