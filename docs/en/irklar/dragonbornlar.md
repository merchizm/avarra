# Dragonborn
<p class="lore-lead">Dragonborn of Odraka are divided between Red Saar and the Gem Oath, rival interpretations of the first dragons' legacy.</p>
<div class="lore-grid"><div class="lore-card"><h3>Red Saar</h3><p>Honour, military service and dragon-blooded houses; capital Saar-Kor.</p></div><div class="lore-card"><h3>Gem Oath</h3><p>Mind discipline and memory crystals; capital Prism.</p></div></div>
