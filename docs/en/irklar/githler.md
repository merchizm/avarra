# Gith

<p class="lore-lead">Despite distant origins, a people rooted in Avarra through sky calendars, mental privacy, and open oaths.</p>

## Place in Avarra

**Far Step** is a small but open settlement in [Asteron’s Northern Passes](../cografya/bolgeler/asteron-kuzey-gecitleri), established around Asteron northern passes. It is not a separate kingdom viewed from outside; it is a village or quarter node tied to neighbouring roads, markets, and common law.

## City life and diaspora

In [Dokuz Fener](../yerlesimler/dokuz-fener), Gith households participate in pass observation and dispute mediation without being confined to a single “foreign quarter.” Someone arriving from the village can find kin, an apprenticeship, a guest bed, or a partner; city-born people maintain regular ties to Far Step.

## Living together

Gith are not reduced to one temperament, profession, or politics. Local agreements, access needs, and safety practices are made explicit so difference remains part of ordinary life. Their visible expertise is **pass observation and dispute mediation**, but it is never anyone’s compulsory destiny.

## Adventure ties

- The partnership between Far Step and Dokuz Fener can be tested by a cut road or a lost record.
- A family may need mediation between local custom and city law.
- A character may belong to this record through a guild, neighbourhood, or road oath as well as ancestry.
