#Aboleths
 <p class="lore-lead"> Ancient intelligent beings of the deep waters who claim that their memories are older than those of gods and humanoids. </p>
 <p> This claim is not proven; However, some Aboleths have been observed knowing lost coastlines and forgotten languages. The past they tell is as much bargaining and threat as it is knowledge. </p>

## Canonical record

 <p> Aboleths live in the Silent Deep and some sea trenches. They claim to carry the memories of their ancestors and to exist before the gods. </p>
 <p> States' knowledge of their founding lies and forgotten coastlines is more dangerous than their physical power. There is no common Aboleth empire in Avarra; each individual acts as an ancient memory dynasty. </p>
