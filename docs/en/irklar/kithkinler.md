# Kithkin

<p class="lore-lead">A cultural lineage within halfling kinships that sustains mutual care and common tables at an extended-family scale.</p>

## Place in Avarra

**Seventh Crop** is a small but open settlement in [Wind–Reed Corridor](../cografya/bolgeler/odraka-ruzgar-saz), established around Odraka farming corridor. It is not a separate kingdom viewed from outside; it is a village or quarter node tied to neighbouring roads, markets, and common law.

## City life and diaspora

In [Sazkalp](../yerlesimler/sazkalp), Kithkin households participate in seed exchange, hospitality, and neighbourhood kitchens without being confined to a single “foreign quarter.” Someone arriving from the village can find kin, an apprenticeship, a guest bed, or a partner; city-born people maintain regular ties to Seventh Crop.

## Living together

Kithkin are not reduced to one temperament, profession, or politics. Local agreements, access needs, and safety practices are made explicit so difference remains part of ordinary life. Their visible expertise is **seed exchange, hospitality, and neighbourhood kitchens**, but it is never anyone’s compulsory destiny.

## Adventure ties

- The partnership between Seventh Crop and Sazkalp can be tested by a cut road or a lost record.
- A family may need mediation between local custom and city law.
- A character may belong to this record through a guild, neighbourhood, or road oath as well as ancestry.
