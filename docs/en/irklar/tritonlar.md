# Tritons
 <p class="lore-lead"> Guardian population of the military order around sea pits and monster migration routes. </p>

## Canonical record

 <p> The Triton Deep Watch is a military order that guards the sea trenches and great monster migration routes. They consider themselves the protectors of the world against threats unknown to the surface peoples. </p>
 <p> This mission is based on real threats; but it also provides justification for the Tritons to act without consulting the coastal states. Their wars with the Sahuagin are regular, ancient, and at times diplomatic. </p>
