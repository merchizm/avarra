# Hexbloods
 <p class="lore-lead"> Fey, whose life has been changed by ancient magic or witch pacts; people defined as much by their choices as by their origins. </p>

## Transformation, not citizenship

A Hexblood’s Fey touch does not automatically make them a member of a free court or a citizen of Rootbeyond. The transformation may concern a witch, a First Fey, a living grove, or a personal oath. The character’s real question is not “which court am I from?” but to whom the surviving word of their transformation remains binding.

## Canonical record

 <p> Hexbloods are individuals transformed by witches, Fey, or living forest pacts. May be protected by the Pale Circle in the Crown of Thorns; Ordan is also considered an unregistered magic carrier. </p>
 <p> A Hexblood does not lose his ancient culture. Whether family ties will continue after the transformation depends on the law of the society. Some start their own small covenant families. </p>
