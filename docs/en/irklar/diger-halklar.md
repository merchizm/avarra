# Other Peoples
<p class="lore-lead">This compendium collects smaller, dispersed or multi-centred peoples named by the atlas. Each heading is a canonical starting point for a later independent record.</p>
## Elven peoples
<p>Wood Elves shape the Thorn Crown, High Elves shape Lethar, and Sea Elves defend maritime rights through the Coral Courts.</p>
## Firbolgs and Thornbloods
<p>Root Firbolgs protect sacred groves and burial forests. Thornbloods form a major part of the Thorn Crown's army and seek equal citizenship.</p>
## Underground and sea peoples
<p>Duergar, Myconids, Locathah, Kuo-Toa, Tritons and Sahuagin each hold distinct labour, memory, water and deep-sea legal traditions.</p>
## Other lineages
<p>Tortles, Centaurs, Tabaxi, Kenku, Aarakocra, Owlin, Orcs, Hobgoblins, Reborn, Hexbloods, Dhampirs and Autognomes are all represented in the atlas's cultural networks.</p>
