# Shadar-kai

<p class="lore-lead">Shadow Elves whose bonds of mourning, memory, and durable craft outlast seasons.</p>

## Shadow witnesses of the Closure

Some Shadar-kai households preserve the names of thresholds lost during the Root Closure. This is neither one exile story nor proof that all Shadar-kai came from one court: most families at Quiet Shore were born in Avarra. Their mourning cloth does not open a gate; it keeps a closed road’s memory equal on both sides.

## Place in Avarra

**Quiet Shore** is a small but open settlement in [Ice Rifts and Brief Summer](../cografya/bolgeler/ilyr-buz-yariklari), established around Ilyr-Khaz shores. It is not a separate kingdom viewed from outside; it is a village or quarter node tied to neighbouring roads, markets, and common law.

## City life and diaspora

In [Kar-Dur](../yerlesimler/kar-dur), Shadar-kai households participate in memory chests, mourning cloth, and winter hosting without being confined to a single “foreign quarter.” Someone arriving from the village can find kin, an apprenticeship, a guest bed, or a partner; city-born people maintain regular ties to Quiet Shore.

## Living together

Shadar-kai are not reduced to one temperament, profession, or politics. Local agreements, access needs, and safety practices are made explicit so difference remains part of ordinary life. Their visible expertise is **memory chests, mourning cloth, and winter hosting**, but it is never anyone’s compulsory destiny.

## Adventure ties

- The partnership between Quiet Shore and Kar-Dur can be tested by a cut road or a lost record.
- A family may need mediation between local custom and city law.
- A character may belong to this record through a guild, neighbourhood, or road oath as well as ancestry.
