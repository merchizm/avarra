# Centaurs


## D&D reference

Centaurs are used as beings of fey or humanoid origin that combine the human body with the horse body. Their strong speed, ability to navigate open terrain, and hoof attack are typical game elements. Practical issues such as stairs, narrow passages, swimming, and human-sized equipment differentiate them in adventure design.

## Canonical record

 <p> Centaurs of the Endless Way consider seasonal right of passage as political sovereignty rather than fixed territory. When a fence, mine or castle blocks the sacred migration route, they consider it an invasion. </p>
 <p> Family is not only blood ties, but also an agreement to share the same herd and road. Permanent cities are few; The Wind Circle serves as the seasonal capital. </p>
