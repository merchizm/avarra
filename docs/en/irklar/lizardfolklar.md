# lizardfolks

 <p class="lore-lead"> Lizardfolk are not callous or primitive. Social politeness is based on body temperature, scent and gifted food rather than facial expression; Water law is also part of ancestral continuity. </p>



## Delta kingdoms

The Great Saz is a canal and agricultural state, the Crocodile Crown is a military frontier kingdom, and the Silent Delta is an order ruled by priests and healers. Long Coast Tortle monasteries remain autonomous within these states.

## Canonical record

 <p> Reed Dragon Lizardfolk are not callous or primitive. They show their kindness through body warmth, scent, posture, and shared food rather than facial expressions. </p>
 <p> They consider wetland temporary and variable. They may consider the river bed fixed with stones as an arrogance towards the environment. The Great Reed is agriculture and canal culture, the Crocodile Crown is defense, the Silent Delta is healing and clerical culture. </p>
