# Wood Elves
 <p class="lore-lead"> Elven branch influential in the foreign policy, archives, and major settlements of the Crown of Thorns. </p>

## Fey connection

Wood Elves read Fey heritage not as proof of belonging to Rootbeyond, but as a long responsibility to the forest. Some archive families in the Crown of Thorns keep records of living thresholds, while frontier groves resist those records becoming a claim of ownership over a gate. A Wood Elf may have dealings with a free court, but it is not citizenship shared by the whole people.

## Canonical record

 <p> The Wood Elves are not a single forest nation. Crown Elves of the Crown of Thorns are strong in government and archival affairs; frontier rangers and independent hunting societies live more locally. </p>
 <p> Due to their long lifespans, they can make a settlement lasting several generations temporary for humans. They measure the impact of treaties in centuries rather than decades. Their closeness to nature does not automatically mean that they are egalitarian; There are families in the Crown of Thorns who defend their political superiority as a sacred order. </p>
