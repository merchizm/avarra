# Drow
<p class="lore-lead">Drow life ranges from the religious monarchy of the Silk Throne to the tax-based citizenship of Dark Harbor and the surface-facing Moonbelow exiles.</p>
<div class="lore-grid"><div class="lore-card"><h3>Silk Throne</h3><p>Vel-Shaar is linked by silk bridges and governed through temple and property law.</p></div><div class="lore-card"><h3>Moonbelow</h3><p>A loose settlement network advocating a return to the surface.</p></div></div>
