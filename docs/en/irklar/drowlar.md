#drow

 <p class="lore-lead"> The Drow are not a single political or religious bloc. The lineage houses of the Silk Throne, the tax law of the Dark Harbor, and the surface politics of the Sublunary exiles are different worlds of the same people. </p>



## Separate paths

The Silk Throne is a religious monarchy; The Dark Harbor defines citizenship through a harbor tax; Sublunar exiles are a loose network of settlements that advocate a return to the surface.

## Canonical record

 <p> Drow are not identified with a single religion or state. </p>
 <p> - <b> Silk Throne: </b> Hierarchical lineage houses based on property and temple law, running through the mother. - <b> Dark Harbor: </b> Multi-ethnic trade, flexible citizenship and religious freedom. - <b> Sublunary Exiles: </b> Communities that consider the return to the surface and the moonlight as a bridge between two worlds. - <b> Ash Weave: </b> Forbidden sect that considers stasis as sacred transformation. </p>
 <p> Drow attunement to darkness is not considered a curse. Communities that emerge to the surface develop new bodies and daily habits. </p>
