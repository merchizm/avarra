# Halflings
 <p class="lore-lead"> Although the Halflings appear to be a single people, they are divided into River, Road and Island branches. Grain, postal, caravan and insurance networks are the common infrastructure that even large states must rely on. </p>

## Canonical record

 <p> Halflings inhabit vast networks ranging from small counties to intercontinental shipping markets. </p>
 <p> - <b> River Halflings: </b> Mill, grain, bank and river transport. - <b> Road Halflings: </b> Caravan, mail and neutral news network. - <b> Island Halflings: </b> Fishing, insurance and maritime trade. </p>
 <p> A house is more than a building, it is a community that shares the same table and road responsibility. Great powers protect Halfling counties not because of their military power but because of their grain and credit networks. </p>
