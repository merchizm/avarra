# Rimekin

<p class="lore-lead">A northern cultural lineage within ice-genasi kinships that manages cold through mutual aid and light knowledge.</p>

## Place in Avarra

**Frozen Light** is a small but open settlement in [Ice Rifts and Brief Summer](../cografya/bolgeler/ilyr-buz-yariklari), established around Ilyr-Khaz fjords. It is not a separate kingdom viewed from outside; it is a village or quarter node tied to neighbouring roads, markets, and common law.

## City life and diaspora

In [Kar-Dur](../yerlesimler/kar-dur), Rimekin households participate in ice lamps, winter seed, and passage watches without being confined to a single “foreign quarter.” Someone arriving from the village can find kin, an apprenticeship, a guest bed, or a partner; city-born people maintain regular ties to Frozen Light.

## Living together

Rimekin are not reduced to one temperament, profession, or politics. Local agreements, access needs, and safety practices are made explicit so difference remains part of ordinary life. Their visible expertise is **ice lamps, winter seed, and passage watches**, but it is never anyone’s compulsory destiny.

## Adventure ties

- The partnership between Frozen Light and Kar-Dur can be tested by a cut road or a lost record.
- A family may need mediation between local custom and city law.
- A character may belong to this record through a guild, neighbourhood, or road oath as well as ancestry.
