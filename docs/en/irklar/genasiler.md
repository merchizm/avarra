# Genasi

<p class="lore-lead">Elemental lineage does not assign a Genasi one profession or class. In Camova, Earth, Fire, Crystal, and Ash communities maintain distinct traditions within one confederation, while water and ice lineages carry their own politics in other watersheds.</p>

## Camova

The confederation, built around glassed plains, obsidian ridges, and mineral veins, gathers its assembly in a different city every five years. The divide between Ordan supporters and independence advocates is deepening.

## Canonical record

<p>Being Genasi is not one ethnicity. Elemental heritage can emerge within a human, Elf, Dwarf, Veyran, or other humanoid family. Camova’s elemental lineages form distinct communities, while Water Genasi of the River Throne live within local current cultures.</p>
<p>Elemental form should not prescribe a person’s work. Although Camova’s old class order treated Earth Genasi as landholders and Fire Genasi as warriors, the modern confederation contests those assumptions.</p>

## Elemental peoples in play

| Lineage | Central tension | Common arena |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Earth | Land, extraction, and the right to close old veins | Camova’s mineral districts |
| Fire | Creation versus the military use of the forge | Obsidian hearths |
| Water | Flow law and control of river access | River Throne canals |
| Ice | What should be preserved, and for whom | Northern records and courts |
| Crystal | Whether memory can hold legal authority | Crystal archives |
| Ash | Repair after disaster without repeating old hierarchies | Burned fields and refugee settlements |

<div class="lore-callout">A Genasi’s element is lineage and relationship, not destiny. The same element can belong to a farmer, scholar, sailor, soldier, or dissident.</div>
