# Simic Hybrids

<p class="lore-lead">Citizens who govern bodily adaptations through consent in medicine, safe work, and self-determination.</p>

## Place in Avarra

**Canal Edge** is a small but open settlement in [Meridian Rivers](../cografya/bolgeler/asteron-meridyen-nehirleri), established around Asteron canals. It is not a separate kingdom viewed from outside; it is a village or quarter node tied to neighbouring roads, markets, and common law.

## City life and diaspora

In [Astereth](../yerlesimler/astereth), Simic Hybrids households participate in waterworks, rescue, and accessible design without being confined to a single “foreign quarter.” Someone arriving from the village can find kin, an apprenticeship, a guest bed, or a partner; city-born people maintain regular ties to Canal Edge.

## Living together

Simic Hybrids are not reduced to one temperament, profession, or politics. Local agreements, access needs, and safety practices are made explicit so difference remains part of ordinary life. Their visible expertise is **waterworks, rescue, and accessible design**, but it is never anyone’s compulsory destiny.

## Adventure ties

- The partnership between Canal Edge and Astereth can be tested by a cut road or a lost record.
- A family may need mediation between local custom and city law.
- A character may belong to this record through a guild, neighbourhood, or road oath as well as ancestry.
