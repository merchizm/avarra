# Cultures and Faiths
<p class="lore-lead">The current canon establishes cultural patterns and intentionally leaves named gods and religious law open for the next atlas edition.</p>
<div class="lore-grid"><div class="lore-card"><h3>Seasonal peoples</h3><p>Winter treats protection as the arrest of change; Summer shares twin crowns; Autumn honours loss and relinquishment; Spring practices renewal and debt forgiveness.</p></div><div class="lore-card"><h3>Water law</h3><p>For the River Throne, source, branch, delta, and floodplain belong to one political body. Lizardfolk etiquette reads warmth, scent, and food as carefully as expression.</p></div><div class="lore-card"><h3>Migration and passage</h3><p>The Endless Road Confederacy protects passage rights over fixed property. Halfling road, river, and island branches keep grain, news, and insurance moving.</p></div><div class="lore-card"><h3>Urban cultures</h3><p>Night Quarters, Hollow Quarters, Spore Gardens, Ember Streets, Stateless Edges, and Water Gates recur across mixed cities.</p></div><div class="lore-card"><h3>Stone and labour</h3><p>Kar-Dur hearth families share the mountain by layer; Kor-Dum treats assembly government against forced labour as the basis of citizenship.</p></div><div class="lore-card"><h3>Memory cultures</h3><p>Crystal archivists keep memories in mineral forms; Myconid cities share memory through living networks; Drow exiles make return to the surface a political memory.</p></div><div class="lore-card"><h3>Faith remains open</h3><p>Named gods and religious law await the next canon edition. This index remains the place where future faith records meet culture and urban life.</p></div></div>
