# Cultures and Faiths
<p class="lore-lead">The current canon establishes cultural patterns and intentionally leaves named gods and religious law open for the next atlas edition.</p>
<div class="lore-grid"><div class="lore-card"><h3>Seasonal peoples</h3><p>Winter preserves, Summer shares twin crowns, Autumn honours loss, and Spring practices renewal.</p></div><div class="lore-card"><h3>Urban cultures</h3><p>Night Quarters, Hollow Quarters, Spore Gardens, Ember Streets and Water Gates recur across mixed cities.</p></div></div>
