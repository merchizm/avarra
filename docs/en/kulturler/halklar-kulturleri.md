# Peoples and Cultures Guide
<p class="lore-lead">In Avarra, lineage, culture, citizenship, and social position are distinct layers of identity.</p>
<div class="lore-grid"><div class="lore-card"><h3>Lineage</h3><p>Body and supernatural inheritance.</p></div><div class="lore-card"><h3>Culture</h3><p>Learned values and daily life.</p></div><div class="lore-card"><h3>Citizenship</h3><p>Legal belonging to a state.</p></div><div class="lore-card"><h3>Social position</h3><p>How the world reads a person: noble, refugee, unregistered, clergy, worker.</p></div></div>
<p class="lore-callout">Lineage does not determine morality. Members of one people can be citizens of rival states, followers of different faiths, and occupy opposing social positions.</p>
