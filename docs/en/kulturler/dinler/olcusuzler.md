# Unmeasured

<p class="lore-lead">The Unmeasured grow Kharûmi practice’s most dangerous error: they treat every human being as a piece to use in solving a knot.</p>

Most do not call themselves Unmeasured. They use names such as Long Account, Silent Scale, or Necessary Hand. They share the belief that great order is worth more than small people’s freedom. They will hasten war, bankruptcy, family conflict, or a hidden scandal if they think a larger stability will follow.

## Making knots

For a Kharûmi, a knot is a wound already in the world. For the Unmeasured, it is an opportunity that can be created to prove oneself. A port strike is made to break sooner, two heirs are directed to the same document, grain rumour is enlarged through a city, and restoring order is presented as success.

They are rarely obvious villains. They may be patient, helpful, educated, and kind. That is the danger: they do not count the harm they cause as real harm, only as a number paid in an account.

When a house suspects a cell has become Unmeasured, it does not first seek to seize secret knowledge. It examines the old knots the cell claims to have solved: who gained, who vanished, and who was later left with debt or grief. Without repair, there was no measure.

Eshrenic Open Witnesses, Theskarite grief houses, and local assemblies often join such inquiries. The Unmeasured do not use darkness. They use the story everyone tells themselves about the “necessary decision.”
