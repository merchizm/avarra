# The Living Whole

<p class="lore-lead">Avarra is itself a living, sacred whole: mountains its bones, rivers its veins, and forests its memory.</p>
<p>Birth is separation, life is taking form, and death is return. Its traditions are the Root Compact, First Echo, Flowing Way, and Last Hearth.</p>
