# Seal Order of Ordan
 <p class="lore-lead"> It is not a separate religion; It is an imperial institution that records temples, clergy, revenue, and resurrection rites. </p>
 <p> What is forbidden is unregistered divine authority rather than faith. A forest spirit may be lawful; Large funeral organizations independent of the state can be considered a threat. </p>
