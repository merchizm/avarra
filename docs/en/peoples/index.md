# Peoples and Lineages

<p class="lore-lead">No people in Avarra is defined by a single culture or state. Law, language, economy, memory and government shape belonging more strongly than ancestry.</p>

<div class="lore-grid">
  <div class="lore-card"><h3><a href="./eladrin">Eladrin</a></h3><p>Long-lived fey people whose inner season can shape their voice, face and magic.</p></div>
  <div class="lore-card"><h3>Veyrans</h3><p>The founding people of Ordan, known for double-ringed irises and the natural lines on their hands and necks.</p></div>
  <div class="lore-card"><h3>Nimrûn</h3><p>Svirfneblin people of the Nine Lanterns; they believe they emerged from echoes held inside stone.</p></div>
  <div class="lore-card"><h3>Kobolds</h3><p>Underground infrastructure guilds organise their society around shared tunnel safety and maintenance.</p></div>
