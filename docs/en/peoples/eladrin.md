# Eladrin

<p class="lore-lead">Eladrin do not hide their feelings; they live them as seasons. In Avarra, Winter Eladrin of the Frost Court see protection as the preservation of what must not change.</p>

<div class="lore-grid">
  <div class="lore-card"><h3>Homeland</h3><p>Ilyr-Khaz and the northeastern coasts of Nareth.</p></div>
  <div class="lore-card"><h3>Known court</h3><p>The Frost Court, ruled from Nivarra.</p></div>
  <div class="lore-card"><h3>Political tie</h3><p>The court supports Prince Odran in the Ordan succession conflict.</p></div>
  <div class="lore-card"><h3>Key settlements</h3><p>Nivarra, White Bridge, Shadowless Glass and Last Hearth.</p></div>
